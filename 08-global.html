1. IFRS 17 vs CAS 25 Comparison 国际准则与中国准则对比

English

IFRS 17, issued by the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB), is the global standard for insurance contracts. CAS 25 (Enterprise Accounting Standard No. 25 — Insurance Contracts) is China's corresponding standard, issued by the Ministry of Finance.

The two standards are substantially converged: CAS 25 was designed to align with IFRS 17 while incorporating specific provisions tailored to Chinese market conditions. Understanding the detailed point-by-point comparison is essential for insurers operating across jurisdictions.

中文

IFRS 17 由国际会计准则理事会（IASB）发布，是保险合同的全球准则。CAS 25（企业会计准则第25号——保险合同）是中国对应的准则，由财政部发布。

两项准则实质性趋同：CAS 25 在设计上与 IFRS 17 保持一致，同时纳入了适应中国市场条件的特定条款。对于跨境经营的保险公司而言，理解逐项详细对比至关重要。

IFRS 17 IASB

Key Difference / 关键差异

While CAS 25 is substantially converged with IFRS 17, it includes certain specific provisions reflecting Chinese market conditions — such as phased effective dates for non-listed insurers and transition relief measures. Multinational insurers should consult both standards carefully when preparing group accounts. / 虽然 CAS 25 与 IFRS 17 实质性趋同，但包含若干反映中国市场条件的特定条款，如非上市公司分阶段生效日和过渡缓释措施。跨国保险公司在编制集团报表时应仔细对照两项准则。

2. HKFRS 17 中国香港准则

English

HKFRS 17 is Hong Kong's adoption of IFRS 17, issued by the Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants (HKICPA). It became effective for annual reporting periods beginning on or after January 1, 2023, aligning with the global IFRS 17 effective date.

HKFRS 17 is essentially identical to IFRS 17 with no significant local modifications. Hong Kong's approach is to adopt IFRS standards directly, applying a "copy-on" approach that mirrors the international text.

This is particularly important for insurers operating in the China-Hong Kong market corridor — companies listed in Hong Kong or serving both mainland and Hong Kong policyholders must apply HKFRS 17 for their Hong Kong reporting, while mainland subsidiaries follow CAS 25.

中文

HKFRS 17 是中国香港对 IFRS 17 的采纳版本，由香港会计师公会（HKICPA）发布。该准则于2023年1月1日或之后开始的年度报告期生效，与全球 IFRS 17 生效日保持一致。

HKFRS 17 与 IFRS 17 实质上完全相同，无重大本地修改。香港的做法是直接采纳国际财务报告准则，采用"照搬"方式，原文对应国际文本。

这对于在中国与香港市场走廊经营的保险公司尤为重要——在香港上市或同时服务内地与香港保单持有人的公司，须按照 HKFRS 17 编制香港报告，而内地子公司则遵循 CAS 25。

Cross-Border Reporting / 跨境报告

For insurers with operations in both mainland China and Hong Kong, dual reporting under CAS 25 and HKFRS 17 may be required. Because the two standards are substantially converged, the reconciliation adjustments are expected to be limited, primarily involving disclosure and transition timing differences. / 对于在内地与香港均设有业务的保险公司，可能需要按照 CAS 25 与 HKFRS 17 进行双重报告。由于两项准则实质性趋同，预计调节调整有限，主要涉及披露与过渡时点差异。

3. IFRS 17 vs US GAAP (LDTI) 与美国准则对比

English

US GAAP insurance accounting was significantly revised by ASU 2018-12, known as the Long-Duration Targeted Improvements (LDTI). Issued by the Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB), LDTI represents the most substantial overhaul of US insurance accounting in decades.

While both IFRS 17 and LDTI aim to improve transparency and comparability of long-duration insurance contracts, they take fundamentally different approaches in several key areas — most notably the concept of CSM (which exists in IFRS 17 but not in US GAAP) and the treatment of discount rates.

中文

美国 GAAP 保险会计经 ASU 2018-12（即长险定向改进，LDTI）大幅修订。该准则由财务会计准则委员会（FASB）发布，是对美国保险会计数十年来最重大的改革。

虽然 IFRS 17 与 LDTI 均旨在提高长期保险合同信息的透明度与可比性，但二者在若干关键领域采取了根本不同的方法——尤其是 CSM 概念（IFRS 17 有，US GAAP 无）以及折现率处理方式。

Key Differences: IFRS 17 vs US GAAP (LDTI) 核心差异对比

Aspect / 方面 IFRS 17 US GAAP (LDTI)
Profit Concept
利润概念		 Uses Contractual Service Margin (CSM) — represents unearned profit from the contract, released over coverage period No CSM concept; uses Deferred Acquisition Costs (DAC) and "deferred profit" amortization — profit emerges through DAC unlocking and margin release
Discount Rate
折现率		 Current (updated) discount rate at each reporting date, reflecting market conditions Locked-in (fixed) discount rate at issue for traditional and limited-pay contracts; updated rate only for limited situations (e.g., universal life)
Grouping
分组方法		 Grouping by profitability (onerous / non-onerous / remaining) with the 1-year rule Grouping by issue year + profitability — cohorts defined by issue year and expected profitability at issue
Market Risk Benefits
市场风险给付		 Direct participating contracts measured under Variable Fee Approach (VFA) — CSM adjusts for changes in underlying item fair value Market Risk Benefits (MRB) — a separate reserve measured at fair value with MRB amortization; no equivalent to VFA's CSM mechanism
Effective Date
生效日期		 January 1, 2023 (all insurers globally) LDTI effective 2023 for large (public) filers; 2025 for smaller reporting companies (scaled deferred timeline)
Reinsurance
再保险		 Reinsurance contracts held measured on the same basis as direct business, with CSM for profitable reinsurance Reinsurance measured on a gross basis; no CSM equivalent for ceded business
Risk Adjustment
风险调整		 Explicit risk adjustment for non-financial risk as a component of fulfilment cash flows No explicit risk adjustment; risk margin embedded within the liability for future policy benefits using a premium deficiency check
Fundamental Divergence / 根本差异

The most significant conceptual difference is the CSM: IFRS 17 treats unearned profit as an explicit liability component (CSM) that is amortized over coverage, while US GAAP defers profit through DAC amortization and locked-in assumptions. This leads to materially different profit emergence patterns and balance sheet presentations. / 最显著的概念差异在于 CSM：IFRS 17 将未实现利润作为显性负债组成部分（CSM）在保障期内摊销，而 US GAAP 通过 DAC 摊销与锁定假设递延利润。这导致利润实现节奏与资产负债表列报存在实质性差异。

5. Key Challenges for Insurers 保险公司面临的主要挑战

English

The transition to IFRS 17 (and converged standards) represents one of the largest accounting transformation projects ever undertaken by insurers. The challenges span systems, actuarial models, processes, and strategic decision-making.

中文

向 IFRS 17（及趋同准则）的过渡是保险公司有史以来承担的最大规模会计转型项目之一。挑战涵盖系统、精算模型、流程与战略决策等方面。

1. System & Data Infrastructure
系统与数据基础设施
Requirement for granular contract-level data, new general ledger integration, and high-performance computing for scenario analysis.
2. Actuarial Model Sophistication
精算模型复杂度
Models must support current discount rates, risk adjustment calibration, and CSM roll-forward with full audit trails.
3. CSM Calculation & Roll-forward
CSM 计算与滚存
CSM accumulation and amortization logic is complex; requires identifying future-service vs. past-service changes precisely.
4. Financial Reporting Process
财务报告流程变革
New income statement structure (insurance service result vs. finance result), new disclosures, and faster close cycles.
5. Asset-Liability Matching
资产负债匹配
Current discount rates and IFIE volatility require revisiting ALM strategy and investment portfolio construction.
6. OCI Choice Strategy
OCI 选择策略
The IFIE split between P&L and OCI is an accounting policy choice with lasting P&L volatility and equity implications.
Strategic Implication / 战略影响

The OCI choice (IFIE split) is irrevocable once selected and affects comparability across peers. Insurers must align this decision with their overall risk management and investor communication strategy. Early engagement with auditors and regulators is essential. / OCI 选择权（IFIE 拆分）一经选定不可变更，并影响同业可比性。保险公司须将此决策与整体风险管理与投资者沟通策略保持一致。尽早与审计师及监管机构沟通至关重要。
