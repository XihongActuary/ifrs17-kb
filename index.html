💡 Why Bilingual Learning? 为什么要中英文对照学习？

English

IFRS 17 is an international standard originally written in English. The official text, implementation guidance, and global discussions all use English terminology. Mastering the English vocabulary enables you to:

  • Read the original standard directly without translation delay
  • Communicate with international peers and auditors
  • Access global research papers and industry reports
  • Understand the rationale behind each requirement
中文

IFRS 17 是国际准则，原文为英文。官方文本、实施指引和全球讨论均使用英文术语。掌握英文词汇能够帮助你：

  • 直接阅读准则原文，无需等待翻译
  • 与国际同行和审计师交流
  • 获取全球研究论文和行业报告
  • 理解每项要求背后的逻辑和原理

📊 Core Concepts at a Glance 核心概念速览

English Term 中文术语 Abbr. One-line Definition
Insurance Contract 保险合同 - A contract under which one party accepts significant insurance risk from another party
Fulfilment Cash Flows 履约现金流 FCF Probability-weighted estimate of future net cash flows + risk adjustment
Contractual Service Margin 合同服务边际 CSM Unearned profit of a group of insurance contracts
Risk Adjustment 非金融风险调整 RA Compensation for uncertainty about amount and timing of cash flows
Building Block Approach 通用模型（要素法） BBA General measurement model: FCF + CSM
Variable Fee Approach 浮动收费法 VFA Measurement model for direct participating contracts
Premium Allocation Approach 保费分配法 PAA Simplified model for short-duration contracts (≤ 1 year)
Coverage Unit 保障责任单元 CU Basis for CSM amortization over coverage period
Liability for Remaining Coverage 未到期责任负债 LRC Liability attributable to future service
Liability for Incurred Claims 已发生赔款负债 LIC Liability attributable to past service
