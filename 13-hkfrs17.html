HKFRS 17 Insurance Contracts

香港财务报告准则第17号——保险合同
HKICPA 23 Sections More Complete Than IFRS17 Page 18 IE Links Jan 2023 HK-Specific Guidance
About This Page | 关于本页

HKFRS 17（香港财务报告准则第17号——保险合同）由香港会计师公会（HKICPA）发布，与IFRS 17实质等同。本页内容比IFRS 17摘要页更完整：包含23个章节（IFRS 17页为16个），增加了保费分配法、持有再保险、取得合同、保险金融损益等专节，以及香港特有实施指引和完整示例索引。涉及示例的内容通过可点击链接关联至示例对照页。
HKFRS 17 is substantially identical to IFRS 17, issued by HKICPA. This page is more comprehensive than the IFRS 17 summary page, with 23 sections (vs 16), dedicated sections for PAA, Reinsurance Held, Acquired Contracts, and IFIE, plus HK-specific guidance and a full IE index with clickable links to the Illustrative Examples page.

Section 1: Objective 第一节 目标
Paras 1–2 ▶
HKFRS 17 sets out the objective of providing relevant information that faithfully represents insurance contracts. This section is identical to IFRS 17 but is issued by the Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants (HKICPA).
Para 1 第一段 Objective / 目标
English Original

The objective of HKFRS 17 is to ensure that an entity provides relevant information that faithfully represents those contracts. This information gives a basis for users of financial statements to assess the effect that insurance contracts have on the entity's financial position, financial performance and cash flows.

中文翻译

HKFRS 17的目标是确保主体提供能如实反映保险合同的相关信息。这些信息为财务报表使用者评估保险合同对主体财务状况、财务业绩和现金流量的影响提供了基础。

Para 2 第二段 Substance over Form / 实质重于形式
English Original

To achieve that objective, this Standard applies accounting treatment that reflects the economic substance of contracts rather than their legal form. An entity shall consider all terms and conditions of the contract, including those that may affect the amount or timing of cash flows, in determining the substance of the contract.

中文翻译

为实现该目标，本准则采用的会计处理反映合同的经济实质而非法律形式。主体在确定合同实质时，应当考虑合同的所有条款和条件，包括那些可能影响现金流量金额或时间的条款和条件。

Section 2: Scope 第二节 范围
Paras 3–8 ▶
HKFRS 17 applies to insurance contracts issued, reinsurance contracts held, and investment contracts with discretionary participation features. References to other standards use HKFRS/HKAS nomenclature.
Para 3 第三段 Scope of Application / 适用范围
English Original

An entity shall apply HKFRS 17 to: (a) insurance contracts, including reinsurance contracts, it issues; (b) reinsurance contracts it holds; and (c) investment contracts with discretionary participation features it issues, provided the entity also issues insurance contracts.

中文翻译

主体应当将HKFRS 17适用于：(a)其签发的保险合同（含再保险合同）；(b)其持有的再保险合同；以及(c)其签发的具有相机参与分红特征的投资合同，前提是主体同时签发保险合同。

Para 4 第四段 References to Reinsurance and DPF / 再保险与DPF投资合同的引用
English Original

References in this Standard to insurance contracts issued and reinsurance contracts held shall also apply to investment contracts with discretionary participation features issued by an entity that also issues insurance contracts. In Hong Kong, such investment contracts are commonly issued by life insurers with participating ("with-profits") products.

中文翻译

本准则中关于签发的保险合同和持有的再保险合同的引用，同样适用于同时签发保险合同的主体的具有相机参与分红特征的投资合同。在香港，此类投资合同通常由签发分红（"with-profits"）产品的人寿保险公司发行。

Para 7 第七段 Insurance Risk Definition / 保险风险定义
English Original

A contract is an insurance contract only if it transfers significant insurance risk. Insurance risk is risk other than financial risk, accepted from another party. Significant insurance risk is present if an insured event could cause the issuer to pay significant additional benefits in any scenario, excluding scenarios that have no commercial substance.

中文翻译

仅当合同转移了重大保险风险时，该合同才是保险合同。保险风险是指从另一方承担的除金融风险以外的风险。如果保险事项在任何具有商业实质的情形下都可能导致签发人支付重大额外利益，则存在重大保险风险。

Para 8 第八段 Exclusions / 排除事项
English Original

This Standard does not apply to: (a) warranties issued directly by a manufacturer, retailer or dealer; (b) employers' assets and liabilities from employee benefit plans; (c) contingent consideration in a business combination; and (d) direct insurance contracts that the entity holds (ie as a policyholder).

中文翻译

本准则不适用于：(a)制造商、零售商或经销商直接签发的产品质保；(b)雇主因雇员福利计划形成的资产和负债；(c)企业合并中的或有对价；以及(d)主体直接持有（即作为保单持有人）的直接保险合同。

Section 3: Combining Contracts 第三节 合同合并
Para 9 ▶
Contracts entered into at the same time with the same counterparty may need to be combined into a single contract for accounting purposes.
Para 9 第九段 Combining Contracts / 合同合并
English Original

An entity shall combine a portfolio of insurance contracts into a single contract for accounting purposes if the contracts are entered into at the same time with the same counterparty, and the contracts are such that the entity's rights and obligations under each contract are not separate from its rights and obligations under the other contracts in the portfolio.

中文翻译

如果保险合同组合中的合同是与同一对手方同时订立的，且主体在每个合同下的权利和义务不能与该组合中其他合同下的权利和义务相分离，则主体应当出于会计目的将该组合合并为一个单一合同。

Section 4: Contract Boundary 第四节 合同边界
Paras 10–11 ▶
The contract boundary determines which future cash flows are included in the measurement of insurance contracts.
Para 10 第十段 Contract Boundary Principle / 合同边界原则
English Original

An entity shall assess whether cash flows are within the boundary of an insurance contract by considering the substantive rights and obligations of the entity and the policyholder at the reporting date.

中文翻译

主体应当通过考虑报告日主体和保单持有人的实质性权利和义务来评估现金流量是否在保险合同边界之内。

Para 11 第十一段 Inclusion of Future Cash Flows / 未来现金流量纳入
English Original

An entity shall include in the measurement of a group of insurance contracts all the future cash flows within the boundary of each contract in the group. Future cash flows are within the contract boundary if: (a) the entity has a substantive obligation to provide coverage, resulting in cash flows; or (b) the entity has a practical ability to reassess the risks of the policyholder and set a price or level of benefits for that coverage.

中文翻译

主体应当将每个合同边界内的所有未来现金流量纳入保险合同组的计量。当满足以下条件时，未来现金流量在合同边界内：(a)主体有实质性义务提供保障，从而产生现金流量；或(b)主体有实际能力重新评估保单持有人的风险并为该保障设定价格或给付水平。

Section 5: Separation of Components 第五节 分拆
Paras 12–13 ▶
HKFRS 17 requires separation of embedded derivatives, investment components, and goods/non-insurance services from the host insurance contract. The Illustrative Examples provide practical guidance on separation.
Para 12 第十二段 Embedded Derivatives / 嵌入衍生工具
English Original

An entity shall separate an embedded derivative from a host insurance contract and account for it as a derivative in accordance with HKFRS 9 if, and only if, the embedded derivative's economic characteristics are not closely related to those of the host contract.

中文翻译

当且仅当嵌入衍生工具的经济特征与主保险合同的经济特征不密切相关时，主体应当将嵌入衍生工具从主保险合同中分拆，并按照HKFRS 9作为衍生工具进行会计处理。

Para 13 第十三段 Investment Components and Goods/Services / 投资成分与商品服务
English Original

An entity shall separate from a host insurance contract any investment component if that component is not integral to the host contract. An entity shall separate promised goods or non-insurance services from a host insurance contract if the entity can sell those goods or services separately.

中文翻译

如果投资成分与主合同不是不可分割的，主体应当将该投资成分从主保险合同中分拆。如果主体能够单独销售商品或非保险服务，主体应当将承诺的商品或非保险服务从主保险合同中分拆。

Section 6: Level of Aggregation 第六节 汇聚层级
Paras 14–19 ▶
Contracts must be grouped into portfolios, profitability groups, and annual cohorts.
Para 14 第十四段 Portfolios / 保险合同组合
English Original

An entity shall group contracts into portfolios comprising contracts subject to similar risks and managed together. Contracts are not subject to similar risks if they are subject to different types of risk, or if the terms of the contracts would result in significantly different profiles of cash flows.

中文翻译

主体应当将合同分组为包含面临相似风险且一起管理的合同的组合。如果合同面临不同类型的风险，或者合同条款会导致显著不同的现金流量特征，则该等合同不面临相似风险。

Para 15 第十五段 Profitability Groups / 盈利能力分组
English Original

An entity shall divide a portfolio of insurance contracts issued into a minimum of: (a) a group of contracts that are onerous at initial recognition; (b) a group that at initial recognition have no significant possibility of becoming onerous subsequently; and (c) a group of the remaining contracts in the portfolio.

中文翻译

主体应当将签发的保险合同组合至少分为：(a)初始确认时亏损的合同组；(b)初始确认时没有显著可能随后变为亏损的合同组；以及(c)该组合中剩余的合同组。

Para 16 第十六段 Annual Cohort Constraint / 年度批次限制
English Original

An entity shall not include contracts issued more than one year apart in the same group. This annual cohort requirement ensures that the profitability of contracts is not distorted by mixing contracts from different vintages.

中文翻译

主体不得将发行间隔超过一年的合同纳入同一合同组。该年度批次要求确保合同的盈利能力不会因混合不同年份的合同而失真。

Para 19 第十九段 Annual Cohorts / 年度批次
English Original

Each of the groups described in paragraph 15 shall comprise contracts issued in the same annual period (an annual cohort), unless an entity determines that grouping contracts issued over a longer period would result in groups that are not materially different.

中文翻译

第十五段所述的每个合同组应当包含同一年度期间内发行的合同（一个年度批次），除非主体确定将更长期间内发行的合同分组不会导致重大不同的合同组。

Section 7: Recognition 第七节 确认
Paras 20–24 ▶
HKFRS 17 specifies when to initially recognise and when to derecognise groups of insurance contracts and reinsurance contracts held.
Para 20 第二十段 Initial Recognition / 初始确认
English Original

An entity shall recognise a group of insurance contracts it issues from the earliest of: (a) the beginning of the coverage period of the group of contracts, including any coverage relating to contracts in the group that have not yet commenced; (b) the date when the first payment from a policyholder in the group becomes due; and (c) for a group of onerous contracts, the date when the group becomes onerous.

中文翻译

主体应当自以下最早日起确认其签发的保险合同组：(a)该合同组的保障期开始日，包括该组中尚未开始的合同所涉及的保障；(b)该组中保单持有人首期付款到期日；以及(c)对于亏损合同组，该组变为亏损的日期。

Para 22 第二十二段 Derecognition / 终止确认
English Original

An entity shall derecognise a group of insurance contracts at the earlier of: (a) the date when the rights and obligations in the group are extinguished (i.e. discharged, cancelled or expired); or (b) the date when the rights and obligations in the group are substantially modified such that the modified contract would be treated as a new contract.

中文翻译

主体应当在以下较早的日期终止确认保险合同组：(a)该组中的权利和义务被消灭（即解除、取消或到期）的日期；或(b)该组中的权利和义务被重大修改，使得修改后的合同被视为新合同的日期。

Para 24 第二十四段 Reinsurance Held Recognition / 持有再保险的确认
English Original

An entity shall recognise a group of reinsurance contracts held from the earlier of: (a) the beginning of the coverage period of the group of reinsurance contracts held; or (b) the date when the first payment to the issuer of the reinsurance contracts becomes due.

中文翻译

主体应当自以下较早的日期确认持有的再保险合同组：(a)该持有再保险合同组的保障期开始日；或(b)向再保险合同签发人支付首期款项到期日。

Section 8: Initial Measurement 第八节 初始计量
Paras 25–34 ▶
At initial recognition, a group of insurance contracts is measured as the sum of fulfilment cash flows and the contractual service margin (CSM). The Illustrative Examples demonstrate the initial measurement of both onerous and profitable groups.
Para 25 第二十五段 Initial Measurement / 初始计量
English Original

At initial recognition, an entity shall measure a group of insurance contracts at the sum of: (a) the fulfilment cash flows, comprising: (i) estimates of future cash flows; (ii) the discount rate; and (iii) the risk adjustment for non-financial risk; and (b) the contractual service margin.

中文翻译

初始确认时，主体应当按照下列各项之和对保险合同组进行计量：(a)履约现金流量，包括：(i) 未来现金流量的估计值；(ii)折现率；以及(iii) 非金融风险的风险调整；以及(b)合同服务边际。

📊 Example 1: Measurement on Initial Recognition | 示例1——初始确认计量

Para 26 第二十六段 Estimates of Future Cash Flows / 未来现金流量估计
English Original

Estimates of future cash flows shall incorporate all of the following, in an unbiased way: (a) all cash flows within the boundary of the contract; (b) a probability-weighted average of the full range of possible outcomes, reflecting both the amount and timing of cash flows; and (c) current estimates, updated at each reporting date.

中文翻译

未来现金流量的估计值应当以不偏不倚的方式纳入以下各项：(a)合同边界内的所有现金流量；(b)所有可能结果的概率加权平均值，反映现金流量的金额和时间；以及(c)当前估计值，并在每个报告日更新。

Para 29 第二十九段 Risk Adjustment / 风险调整
English Original

The risk adjustment for non-financial risk represents the compensation the entity requires for bearing the uncertainty about the amount and timing of the cash flows that arises from non-financial risk. The risk adjustment shall: (a) reflect all non-financial risk associated with the group of contracts; (b) be determined in a way that is consistent with the amount the entity would require as compensation for bearing that risk.

中文翻译

非金融风险的风险调整代表主体因承担非金融风险导致的现金流量金额和时间不确定性所要求的补偿。风险调整应当：(a)反映与该合同组相关的所有非金融风险；(b)以与主体为承担该风险所要求的补偿金额一致的方式确定。

Para 30 第三十段 Discount Rate / 折现率
English Original

The discount rate shall: (a) reflect the time value of money, the characteristics of the cash flows and the liquidity characteristics of the insurance contracts; (b) be consistent with observable current market prices for instruments with cash flows whose currency, timing and risk profile are consistent with those of the insurance contracts; and (c) not be adjusted to reflect the credit risk of the entity or the credit risk of any related assets held.

中文翻译

折现率应当：(a)反映货币时间价值、现金流量特征以及保险合同的流动性特征；(b)与现金流量币种、时间和风险特征与保险合同一致的工具的可观察当前市场价格保持一致；以及(c)不因反映主体信用风险或持有的任何相关资产的信用风险而进行调整。

Para 32 第三十二段 CSM Definition / 合同服务边际定义
English Original

The contractual service margin represents the unearned profit the entity will recognise as it provides services in the future. An entity shall not recognise a contractual service margin that is a net cost. Instead, the entity shall recognise that net cost as a loss in profit or loss at initial recognition.

中文翻译

合同服务边际代表主体在未来提供服务时将确认的未实现利润。主体不得确认代表净成本的合同服务边际。相反，主体应当在初始确认时将该净成本确认为损失并计入当期损益。

Para 33 第三十三段 Day-1 Loss / 首日损失
English Original

If at initial recognition the sum of the fulfilment cash flows for a group is a net outflow, the entity shall recognise the excess as a loss in profit or loss. This loss represents a day-1 loss, arising when the estimated future cash outflows exceed the estimated future cash inflows at initial recognition.

中文翻译

如果在初始确认时合同组的履约现金流量之和为净流出，主体应当将超过部分确认为损失并计入当期损益。该损失代表首日损失，发生于初始确认时估计的未来现金流出超过估计的未来现金流入的情形。

Para 34 第三十四段 Acquisition Cash Flows / 保险获取现金流量
English Original

An entity shall include in the fulfilment cash flows the acquisition cash flows arising from the initial recognition of the group of contracts. Acquisition cash flows are cash flows paid to or received from parties to the contract (or to parties that have a close relationship with the contract) that vary with the number or characteristics of the contracts.

中文翻译

主体应当将合同组初始确认时产生的保险获取现金流量纳入履约现金流量。保险获取现金流量是支付给或从合同对手方（或与合同有密切关系的对手方）收到并随合同数量或特征而变化的现金流量。

Section 9: Subsequent Measurement — LRC 第九节 后续计量——未到期责任负债
Paras 35–45 ▶
The liability for remaining coverage (LRC) is measured at each reporting date as the sum of fulfilment cash flows allocated to remaining coverage and the CSM. The Illustrative Examples provide detailed year-by-year reconciliation tables.
Para 35 第三十五段 Insurance Contract Liability / 保险合同负债
English Original

An entity shall measure a group of insurance contracts at each reporting date as the sum of: (a) the liability for remaining coverage (excluding any loss component), comprising: (i) the fulfilment cash flows allocated to the remaining coverage; and (ii) the contractual service margin; and (b) the liability for incurred claims.

中文翻译

主体应当在每个报告日将保险合同组计量为以下各项之和：(a)未到期责任负债（不含亏损成分），包括：(i) 分配至剩余保障的履约现金流量；以及(ii)合同服务边际；以及(b)已发生赔款负债。

Para 38 第三十八段 CSM Roll-Forward (Non-DPF) / 合同服务边际滚动（非DPF）
English Original

For groups without direct participation features, the contractual service margin shall be adjusted at each reporting date for: (a) new contracts added to the group; (b) interest accreted on the contractual service margin using the locked-in discount rate; (c) changes in fulfilment cash flows relating to future service; (d) the effect of foreign exchange differences; and (e) amounts amortised based on coverage units as services are provided.

中文翻译

对于不具有直接参与分红特征的合同组，合同服务边际应当在每个报告日按以下各项进行调整：(a)加入该组的新合同；(b)使用锁定折现率对合同服务边际计提的利息；(c)与未来服务相关的履约现金流量变动；(d)外汇汇兑差异的影响；以及(e)按照保障责任单元在提供服务时摊销的金额。

Para 39 第三十九段 Onerous Contracts — Loss Component / 亏损合同——亏损成分
English Original

If a group of contracts is onerous at initial recognition, or becomes onerous subsequently, an entity shall determine a loss component for the group. The loss component is measured at the amount of the excess of the fulfilment cash flows over the contractual service margin (zero if there is no CSM). Changes in the loss component shall be recognised in profit or loss.

中文翻译

如果合同组初始确认时亏损，或随后变为亏损，主体应当为该组确定亏损成分。亏损成分按照履约现金流量超过合同服务边际（若无CSM则为零）的金额计量。亏损成分的变动应当计入当期损益。

Para 40 第四十段 Coverage Units / 保障责任单元
English Original

To determine the amount of the contractual service margin to recognise in profit or loss for a period, an entity shall determine the quantity of coverage provided by a group of insurance contracts. The quantity of coverage shall be determined by considering, for each policy, the amount of coverage and the period of coverage.

中文翻译

为确定当期应确认计入损益的合同服务边际金额，主体应当确定保险合同组提供的保障责任单元数量。保障责任单元数量应当通过考虑每份保单的保障金额和保障期间来确定。

Para 41 第四十一段 CSM Amortization / 合同服务边际摊销
English Original

An entity shall recognise in profit or loss the amount of the contractual service margin in a group of insurance contracts in each period to reflect the services provided under the contracts in that period, determined by reference to the coverage units of the group.

中文翻译

主体应当在每个期间将保险合同组的合同服务边际金额确认计入当期损益，以反映该期间合同下提供的服务，金额参照该组的保障责任单元确定。

Section 10: Subsequent Measurement — LIC 第十节 后续计量——已发生赔款负债
Paras 46–47 ▶
The liability for incurred claims (LIC) represents the entity's obligation to pay claims that have already been incurred, including IBNR.
Para 46 第四十六段 Liability for Incurred Claims / 已发生赔款负债
English Original

The liability for incurred claims comprises the part of the insurance contract liability that relates to expired coverage. The LIC represents the entity's obligation to pay claims that have already been incurred, including claims that have been reported and those that have been incurred but not yet reported (IBNR).

中文翻译

已发生赔款负债包括保险合同负债中与已到期保障相关的部分。已发生赔款负债代表主体对已发生赔款的支付义务，包括已报告赔款和已发生未报告赔款（IBNR）。

Para 47 第四十七段 Subsequent Changes to LIC / 已发生赔款负债的后续变动
English Original

An entity shall measure the liability for incurred claims at the fulfilment cash flows allocated to the expired coverage at the reporting date. Changes in the liability for incurred claims shall be recognised as insurance service expenses or insurance finance income and expenses, as appropriate.

中文翻译

主体应当按照报告日分配至已到期保障的履约现金流量计量已发生赔款负债。已发生赔款负债的变动应当根据情况确认为保险服务费用或保险金融损益。

Section 11: CSM for DPF (VFA) 第十一节 具有直接参与分红特征的合同服务边际（变动收费法）
Paras 45, 86–88 ▶
The Variable Fee Approach (VFA) applies to insurance contracts with direct participation features. Under VFA, changes in the entity's share of the fair value of underlying items adjust the CSM. The Illustrative Examples provide detailed VFA measurement illustrations.
Para 45 第四十五段 Variable Fee Approach / 变动收费法
English Original

An entity shall apply the variable fee approach to a group of insurance contracts with direct participation features if, at initial recognition: (a) the contractual terms specify that the policyholder participates in a share of a clearly identified pool of underlying items; (b) the entity expects to pay to the policyholder an amount equal to a substantial share of the fair value returns on the underlying items; and (c) a substantial proportion of any change in the amounts to be paid to the policyholder is expected to vary with the change in fair value of the underlying items.

中文翻译

主体应当对具有直接参与分红特征的保险合同组适用变动收费法，如果初始确认时满足以下条件：(a)合同条款规定保单持有人参与清晰可辨认的基础项目池的份额；(b)主体预期向保单持有人支付的金额等于基础项目公允价值回报的重大份额；以及(c)预期向保单持有人支付金额的任何变动中，重大比例随基础项目公允价值的变动而变动。

Para 87 第八十七段 Changes in Entity's Share / 主体份额变动
English Original

Under the variable fee approach, changes in the entity's share of the fair value of underlying items shall adjust the contractual service margin. The entity's share is the fair value of underlying items less the fulfilment cash flows that do not vary with the underlying items.

中文翻译

在变动收费法下，主体享有的基础项目公允价值份额的变动应当调整合同服务边际。主体份额是指基础项目公允价值减去不随基础项目变动的履约现金流量后的金额。

Para 88 第八十八段 Risk Management Mitigation / 风险管理缓释
English Original

An entity may, for groups of contracts with direct participation features, include in the contractual service margin changes in the entity's share of the fair value of underlying items that relate to risk mitigation by derivatives, or reinsurance contracts held, used to manage financial risk.

中文翻译

对于具有直接参与分红特征的合同组，主体可以将与衍生工具或持有的再保险合同缓释金融风险相关的主体享有的基础项目公允价值份额变动纳入合同服务边际。

Section 12: Premium Allocation Approach (PAA) 第十二节 保费分配法
Paras 53–59 ▶
The Premium Allocation Approach (PAA) is a simplified measurement model for short-duration insurance contracts. This section is more detailed than the IFRS 17 summary page and includes links to the PAA Illustrative Examples.
Para 53 第五十三段 PAA Eligibility / 保费分配法适用条件
English Original

An entity shall apply the premium allocation approach to a group of insurance contracts if: (a) the coverage period of each contract in the group (including coverage provided by all contracts in the group) is 12 months or less; or (b) the entity applies the approach, described in paragraph 55, to the group.

中文翻译

主体应当对保险合同组适用保费分配法，如果：(a)该组中每个合同（包括该组中所有合同提供的保障）的保障期为12个月或更短；或(b)主体对该组适用第55段所述的方法。

Para 55 第五十五段 PAA Simplified Measurement / 保费分配法简化计量
English Original

If the coverage period of a group of contracts is more than 12 months, an entity may apply the premium allocation approach if the entity's assessment, at initial recognition, of the group concludes that the use of the premium allocation approach would not differ materially from the general measurement model. The LRC under PAA comprises: (a) the unearned premium, adjusted for the effect of the time value of money and financial risk; and (b) the insurance service expenses relating to future coverage.

中文翻译

如果合同组的保障期超过12个月，主体在初始确认时评估该组后认为使用保费分配法不会与通用模型产生重大差异的，可以适用保费分配法。保费分配法下的未到期责任负债包括：(a)未到期保费，经货币时间价值和金融风险影响调整；以及(b)与未来保障相关的保险服务费用。

Para 56 第五十六段 LIC under PAA / 保费分配法下的已发生赔款负债
English Original

An entity shall measure the liability for incurred claims applying the same requirements as the general measurement model. The entity may apply a simplified approach for the effect of the time value of money and financial risk if the entity expects that the effect would not be material.

中文翻译

主体应当按照与通用模型相同的要求计量已发生赔款负债。如果主体预期货币时间价值和金融风险的影响不重大，则可以采用简化方法。

Section 13: Reinsurance Contracts Held 第十三节 持有的再保险合同
Paras 60–70 ▶
HKFRS 17 applies specific measurement requirements to reinsurance contracts held. Unlike issued insurance contracts, losses on reinsurance contracts held are recognised immediately and do not create a negative CSM. The Illustrative Examples provide detailed illustrations of both initial and subsequent measurement of reinsurance contracts held.
Para 60 第六十段 Initial Measurement of Reinsurance Held / 持有再保险的初始计量
English Original

An entity shall measure a group of reinsurance contracts held at initial recognition at the sum of: (a) the fulfilment cash flows; and (b) the contractual service margin. However, if the fulfilment cash flows are a net inflow at initial recognition, the entity shall recognise the net inflow as a gain in profit or loss (i.e., no negative CSM is recognised for reinsurance held).

中文翻译

主体应当在初始确认时将持有的再保险合同组计量为以下各项之和：(a)履约现金流量；以及(b)合同服务边际。但是，如果初始确认时履约现金流量为净流入，主体应当将净流入确认为当期损益中的利得（即持有再保险不确认负的合同服务边际）。

Para 66 第六十六段 Subsequent Measurement of Reinsurance Held / 持有再保险的后续计量
English Original

An entity shall adjust the contractual service margin of a group of reinsurance contracts held for changes in the entity's share of the fair value of underlying items, if the group has direct participation features. For groups without DPF, the CSM is adjusted for changes in fulfilment cash flows relating to future service, interest accretion, and amounts recognised in profit or loss based on coverage units.

中文翻译

如果持有再保险合同组具有直接参与分红特征，主体应当按照主体享有的基础项目公允价值份额变动调整持有再保险合同组的合同服务边际。对于不具有直接参与分红特征的合同组，合同服务边际按照与未来服务相关的履约现金流量变动、利息计提以及基于保障责任单元确认计入损益的金额进行调整。

Para 70 第七十段 Loss Recovery for Reinsurance Held / 持有再保险的损失回收
English Original

When a group of underlying insurance contracts is onerous, an entity shall determine a loss recovery component for the group of reinsurance contracts held that provides coverage for the onerous group. The loss recovery represents the amount of the reinsurance contract asset that relates to the recovery of losses on the underlying onerous contracts.

中文翻译

当底层保险合同组亏损时，主体应当为承保该亏损组的再保险合同组确定损失回收成分。损失回收代表与回收底层亏损合同损失相关的再保险合同资产金额。

Section 14: Changes in Estimates 第十四节 估计变更
Paras 89–94 ▶
Changes in fulfilment cash flows related to future service are absorbed by the CSM, while changes related to financial risk are recognised in profit or loss or OCI.
Para 89 第八十九段 Changes in FCF Related to Future Service / 与未来服务相关的履约现金流量变动
English Original

Changes in the fulfilment cash flows relating to future service shall be absorbed by the contractual service margin, and therefore not recognised in profit or loss at the time of the change. Instead, these changes are recognised in profit or loss over the remaining coverage period as the CSM is amortised.

中文翻译

与未来服务相关的履约现金流量变动应当由合同服务边际吸收，因此在变动时不计入当期损益。这些变动将在剩余保障期间随着合同服务边际的摊销而计入损益。

Para 90 第九十段 Financial Risk Changes / 金融风险变动
English Original

Changes in the fulfilment cash flows relating to financial risk are NOT absorbed by the contractual service margin (except for groups of contracts with direct participation features under the variable fee approach). Such changes shall be recognised in profit or loss or other comprehensive income as insurance finance income and expenses.

中文翻译

与金融风险相关的履约现金流量变动不由合同服务边际吸收（在变动收费法下具有直接参与分红特征的合同组除外）。此类变动应当作为保险金融损益计入当期损益或其他综合收益。

Para 94 第九十四段 Changes in Risk Adjustment / 风险调整变动
English Original

Changes in the risk adjustment for non-financial risk shall be recognised in profit or loss. The entity shall present such changes separately as the change in the risk adjustment for non-financial risk, providing users with information about the uncertainty of the amount and timing of cash flows.

中文翻译

非金融风险的风险调整变动应当计入当期损益。主体应当将该等变动作为非金融风险的风险调整变动单独列报，向使用者提供关于现金流量金额和时间不确定性的信息。

Section 15: Presentation 第十五节 列报
Paras 78–86 ▶
HKFRS 17 requires separate presentation of insurance contract assets and liabilities, and separate presentation of insurance service revenue, insurance service expenses, and insurance finance income and expenses in profit or loss. The Illustrative Examples show how these amounts could be presented.
Para 78 第七十八段 Balance Sheet Presentation / 资产负债表列报
English Original

An entity shall present separately in the statement of financial position the carrying amount of groups of: (a) insurance contracts issued that are assets; (b) insurance contracts issued that are liabilities; (c) reinsurance contracts held that are assets; and (d) reinsurance contracts held that are liabilities.

中文翻译

主体应当在资产负债表中单独列报以下各项的账面价值：(a)作为资产的签发保险合同组；(b)作为负债的签发保险合同组；(c)作为资产的持有再保险合同组；以及(d)作为负债的持有再保险合同组。

Para 82 第八十二段 Income Statement Presentation / 利润表列报
English Original

An entity shall present in the statement of profit or loss the amounts arising from insurance contracts, comprising insurance service revenue and insurance service expenses, to reflect the transfer of services provided under the contracts.

中文翻译

主体应当在利润表中列报因保险合同产生的金额，包括保险服务收入和保险服务费用，以反映合同下提供服务的转移。

📊 Example 3: Presentation in the Statement of Profit or Loss | 示例3——利润表中的列报

Para 83 第八十三段 Profit or Loss Items / 损益项目
English Original

An entity shall present separately in profit or loss: (a) insurance service revenue; (b) insurance service expenses; (c) net income from investments (not arising from insurance contracts); and (d) insurance finance income and expenses.

中文翻译

主体应当在损益中单独列报：(a)保险服务收入；(b)保险服务费用；(c)投资净收益（非因保险合同产生）；以及(d)保险金融损益。

Para 85 第八十五段 Insurance Revenue / 保险服务收入
English Original

An entity shall recognise the change in the liability for remaining coverage related to services in the period as insurance revenue. Insurance revenue reflects the amount charged for services provided under the contracts during the period.

中文翻译

主体应当将当期与提供服务相关的未到期责任负债变动确认为保险服务收入。保险服务收入反映当期合同下提供服务所收取的金额。

Para 86 第八十六段 Insurance Finance Income and Expenses (IFIE) / 保险金融损益
English Original

An entity shall present the amount of the change in the carrying amount of the group arising from: (a) the effect of the time value of money and changes in the time value of money; (b) the effect of financial risk; and (c) the amount of the risk adjustment for non-financial risk — as insurance finance income and expenses. An entity may present such amounts in profit or loss or split them between profit or loss and other comprehensive income.

中文翻译

主体应当将合同组账面价值的以下变动作为保险金融损益列报：(a)货币时间价值的影响及其变动的影响；(b)金融风险的影响；以及(c)非金融风险的风险调整金额——主体可以将该等金额计入当期损益，或在当期损益和其他综合收益之间分摊。

Section 16: Disclosure 第十六节 披露
Paras 91–130 ▶
HKFRS 17 has extensive disclosure requirements including reconciliations, risk information, and sensitivity analyses. The disclosure objective is to enable users to assess the effect of insurance contracts on the entity's financial position, performance, and cash flows.
Para 93 第九十三段 Disclosure Objective / 披露目标
English Original

An entity shall disclose information that: (a) gives a basis for users of financial statements to assess the effect that insurance contracts have on the entity's financial position, financial performance and cash flows; and (b) shows the recognised amounts from insurance contracts and the effect of any changes in the estimates used to measure those amounts.

中文翻译

主体应当披露能够实现以下目标的信息：(a)为财务报表使用者评估保险合同对主体财务状况、财务业绩和现金流量影响提供基础；以及(b)显示保险合同产生的已确认金额以及计量该等金额所用估计变更的影响。

Para 100 第一百段 Reconciliation Tables / 调节表
English Original

An entity shall disclose reconciliations that show how the net carrying amounts changed during the period due to: (a) cash flows such as premiums received and claims paid; (b) the recognition of insurance service revenue and insurance service expenses; (c) changes in the contractual service margin; (d) the effect of changes in the discount rate and other financial assumptions; and (e) the effect of foreign exchange differences and acquisitions or disposals.

中文翻译

主体应当披露调节表，列示当期净账面价值的变动如何因以下原因产生：(a)现金流量，如收取的保费和支付的赔款；(b)确认保险服务收入和保险服务费用；(c)合同服务边际变动；(d)折现率及其他金融假设变更的影响；以及(e)外汇汇兑差异和取得或处置的影响。

Para 113 第一百一十三段 Risk Information / 风险信息
English Original

An entity shall disclose information that enables users of its financial statements to evaluate the nature and extent of risks arising from insurance contracts, including: (a) insurance risk, including the sensitivity of profit or loss and equity to changes in relevant variables; (b) concentrations of insurance risk; and (c) information about credit risk, liquidity risk and market risk.

中文翻译

主体应当披露使财务报表使用者能够评估因保险合同产生的风险性质和程度的信息，包括：(a)保险风险，包括损益和所有者权益对相关变量变动的敏感性；(b)保险风险集中度；以及(c)信用风险、流动性风险和市场风险信息。

Para 120 第一百二十段 Sensitivity Analysis / 敏感性分析
English Original

An entity shall disclose a sensitivity analysis that shows how profit or loss and equity would have been affected if changes in the relevant risk variables that were reasonably possible at the end of the reporting period had occurred and had been applied to the conditions existing at that date. The entity shall disclose the methods and assumptions used in preparing the sensitivity analysis.

中文翻译

主体应当披露敏感性分析，列示如果报告期末合理可能发生的相关风险变量变动已发生并应用于该日存在的条件，损益和所有者权益将受到怎样的影响。主体应当披露编制敏感性分析所使用的方法和假设。

Section 17: Transition 第十七节 过渡
Paras C1–C31 ▶
HKFRS 17 provides three transition approaches: full retrospective, modified retrospective, and fair value. The Illustrative Examples provide detailed illustrations of the modified retrospective approach for both non-DPF and DPF contracts.
Para C1 过渡第一段 Retrospective Application / 追溯应用
English Original

An entity shall apply HKFRS 17 retrospectively in accordance with HKAS 8 Accounting Policies, Changes in Accounting Estimates and Errors, except as specified in paragraphs C2–C31. Retrospective application means adjusting the opening balance of each affected component of equity at the date of initial application.

中文翻译

主体应当按照《香港会计准则第8号——会计政策、会计估计变更和差错》追溯应用HKFRS 17，但C2至C31段另有规定的除外。追溯应用是指在首次应用日调整每个受影响权益组成部分的期初余额。

Para C3 过渡第三段 Full Retrospective Approach / 完全追溯法
English Original

Under the full retrospective approach, an entity applies HKFRS 17 to each group of contracts as if HKFRS 17 had always applied, restating comparative information for all prior periods presented. The entity determines the contractual service margin at the date of initial application as if the group had been measured under HKFRS 17 since its inception.

中文翻译

在完全追溯法下，主体对每个合同组追溯应用HKFRS 17，如同HKFRS 17一直适用，并对所有列报的以前期间重述比较信息。主体在首次应用日确定合同服务边际，如同该组自成立以来一直按照HKFRS 17计量。

Para C5 过渡第五段 Modified Retrospective Approach / 修正追溯法
English Original

If full retrospective application is impracticable, an entity shall use a modified retrospective approach. Under this approach, the entity uses reasonable and supportable information to approximate the measurement that would have been produced under the full retrospective approach. The entity shall disclose the reason for using the modified approach and the methods used.

中文翻译

如果完全追溯应用不切实可行，主体应当采用修正追溯法。在该方法下，主体使用合理且可支持的信息来近似完全追溯法下本应产生的计量结果。主体应当披露使用修正法的原因及所使用的方法。

Para C7 过渡第七段 Fair Value Approach / 公允价值法
English Original

If the modified retrospective approach is also impracticable, an entity shall use the fair value approach. Under the fair value approach, the entity measures the group at the fair value of the contracts at the date of initial application and determines the contractual service margin as the difference between that fair value and the fulfilment cash flows at that date.

中文翻译

如果修正追溯法也不切实可行，主体应当采用公允价值法。在公允价值法下，主体在首次应用日按照合同公允价值计量合同组，并将合同服务边际确定为该公允价值与该日履约现金流量之间的差额。

Section 18: Effective Date 第十八节 生效日期
Para C31 ▶
HKFRS 17 is effective for annual reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023, consistent with IFRS 17.
Para C31 过渡第三十一段 Effective Date / 生效日期
English Original

An entity shall apply HKFRS 17 for annual reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023. Earlier application is permitted only if the entity also applies HKFRS 9 and HKFRS 15 on or before the date of initial application of HKFRS 17.

中文翻译

主体应当对2023年1月1日或之后开始的年度报告期间适用HKFRS 17。仅当主体在HKFRS 17首次应用日或之前同时适用HKFRS 9和HKFRS 15时，才允许提前适用。

Section 19: Insurance Contracts Acquired 第十九节 取得的保险合同
Paras 68–70B ▶
HKFRS 17 includes specific guidance for insurance contracts acquired in a business combination or transfer from another entity. This section is unique to the HKFRS 17 page and includes links to the acquired contracts Illustrative Examples.
Para 68 第六十八段 Acquired Contracts — Initial Measurement / 取得合同——初始计量
English Original

An entity shall measure a group of insurance contracts acquired in a business combination or in a transfer of contracts that do not form a business at the amount determined in accordance with HKFRS 3 Business Combinations (or other applicable Standards) at the acquisition date. The entity shall identify the contractual service margin for the acquired group of contracts at that date.

中文翻译

主体应当在取得日按照《香港财务报告准则第3号——业务合并》（或其他适用准则）确定的金额，计量在企业合并中或在不构成业务的合同转让中取得的保险合同组。主体应当在取得日识别该取得合同组的合同服务边际。

Section 20: Insurance Finance Income or Expenses 第二十节 保险金融损益
Paras 81–86, B96–B97 ▶
HKFRS 17 provides specific guidance on the allocation of insurance finance income and expenses between profit or loss and OCI, and on systematic allocation approaches. The Illustrative Examples provide detailed illustrations.
Para 81 第八十一段 IFIE Allocation Options / 保险金融损益分摊选择
English Original

An entity may choose to present insurance finance income and expenses in profit or loss in full, or split them between profit or loss and other comprehensive income. If the entity chooses the OCI option, it shall determine the amount presented in profit or loss using a systematic allocation method.

中文翻译

主体可以选择将保险金融损益全额计入当期损益，或在当期损益和其他综合收益之间分摊。如果主体选择其他综合收益选项，主体应当使用系统分配方法确定计入当期损益的金额。

Para B96 应用指南第B96段 Analysis of Changes in FCF / 履约现金流量变动分析
English Original

An entity shall analyse the source of changes in the fulfilment cash flows to decide whether each change adjusts the contractual service margin. This analysis considers whether changes relate to future service (adjust CSM), current service (recognise in profit or loss), or financial risk (recognise as IFIE).

中文翻译

主体应当分析履约现金流量变动的来源，以决定各项变动是否调整合同服务边际。该分析考虑变动是否与未来服务相关（调整合同服务边际）、当期服务相关（计入当期损益）或金融风险相关（作为保险金融损益确认）。

The Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants (HKICPA) has issued implementation guidance to assist entities in applying HKFRS 17 in the Hong Kong regulatory environment. 香港会计师公会（HKICPA）已发布实施指引，协助主体在香港监管环境中适用HKFRS 17。该指引针对香港保险市场的特定问题，包括分红长期保险业务、与《保险业条例》（第41章）的衔接，以及保险业监管局（IA）的要求。

Para HK2 香港特有第二段 Interaction with HK Regulatory Framework / 与香港监管框架的衔接
English Original

HKFRS 17 operates within the broader Hong Kong insurance regulatory framework. The Insurance Authority (IA) has issued guidance on the interaction between HKFRS 17 and the capital requirements under the Hong Kong Risk-Based Capital (HKRBC) regime. Entities should consider the implications of HKFRS 17 measurement on solvency reporting and ensure consistency between accounting and regulatory measurements where appropriate.

中文翻译

HKFRS 17在更广泛的香港保险监管框架内运作。保险业监管局（IA）已发布关于HKFRS 17与香港风险为本资本（HKRBC）制度下资本要求衔接的指引。主体应当考虑HKFRS 17计量对偿付能力报告的影响，并在适当情况下确保会计计量与监管计量之间的一致性。

Para HK3 香港特有第三段 Participating Long-Term Business / 分红长期保险业务
English Original

For Hong Kong life insurers issuing participating ("with-profits") products, the Variable Fee Approach (VFA) under HKFRS 17 is particularly relevant. The HKICPA guidance addresses how the VFA interacts with the policyholder reasonable expectations (PRE) framework, the management of the participating surplus, and the disclosure of the entity's share of underlying items. The fair value of underlying items may include both financial assets and non-financial components.

中文翻译

对于签发分红（"with-profits"）产品的香港人寿保险公司，HKFRS 17下的变动收费法（VFA）尤为相关。HKICPA指引说明了变动收费法如何与保单持有人合理期望（PRE）框架衔接、如何管理分红盈余池，以及如何披露主体享有的基础项目份额。基础项目的公允价值可能同时包括金融资产和非金融组成部分。

Para HK4 香港特有第四段 Transition Relief for Hong Kong Insurers / 香港保险公司过渡缓释
English Original

Hong Kong insurers transitioning to HKFRS 17 may benefit from specific transition reliefs. These include: (a) the option to use the modified retrospective approach or fair value approach where full retrospective application is impracticable; (b) reliefs for business combination acquisitions; (c) reliefs for grouping contracts; and (d) the option to apply risk mitigation from the date of initial application rather than from inception of the hedging relationship.

中文翻译

过渡到HKFRS 17的香港保险公司可能受益于特定的过渡缓释措施。这些包括：(a)当完全追溯应用不切实可行时，选择使用修正追溯法或公允价值法；(b)企业合并取得的缓释；(c)合同分组的缓释；以及(d)从首次应用日而非套期关系成立日起适用风险管理缓释的选择权。

Section 23: HKFRS 17 vs IFRS 17 Comparison 第二十三节 HKFRS 17与IFRS 17对比分析

Para CMP1 对比第一段 Standard Equivalence / 准则等效性
English Original

HKFRS 17 is substantially identical to IFRS 17 in terms of core principles, measurement models, presentation requirements, and disclosure requirements. The HKICPA adopted IFRS 17 as HKFRS 17 to maintain convergence with international standards. The paragraph numbering is identical.

中文翻译

HKFRS 17在核心原则、计量模型、列报要求和披露要求方面与IFRS 17实质相同。HKICPA将IFRS 17采纳为HKFRS 17，以保持与国际准则的趋同。段编号完全一致。

Para CMP2 对比第二段 Key Differences / 主要差异
English Original

The main differences between HKFRS 17 and IFRS 17 are: (a) references to other standards use HKFRS/HKAS nomenclature (e.g., HKFRS 9, HKFRS 15, HKFRS 3, HKAS 8) instead of IFRS/IAS; (b) HKFRS 17 includes additional Hong Kong-specific implementation guidance from the HKICPA; (c) the effective date is the same (1 January 2023); (d) HKFRS 17 operates within the Hong Kong regulatory framework including the Insurance Ordinance and IA requirements; and (e) the Illustrative Examples accompanying HKFRS 17 are identical to those accompanying IFRS 17.

中文翻译

HKFRS 17与IFRS 17的主要差异为：(a)对其他准则的引用使用HKFRS/HKAS命名（如HKFRS 9、HKFRS 15、HKFRS 3、HKAS 8）而非IFRS/IAS；(b)HKFRS 17包含HKICPA的额外香港特有实施指引；(c)生效日期相同（2023年1月1日）；(d)HKFRS 17在香港监管框架内运作，包括《保险业条例》和IA要求；以及(e)随附HKFRS 17的示例与随附IFRS 17的示例完全一致。
