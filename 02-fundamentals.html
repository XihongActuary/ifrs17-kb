📚 Lesson 1: Fundamentals of IFRS 17 基础概念

Level: L2 Intermediate | Duration: 20 min | Vocabulary: 15 terms

Learning Objectives / 学习目标

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to: (1) explain the background and timeline of IFRS 17; (2) identify the scope of application; (3) describe the contract boundary concept; (4) explain contract separation and disaggregation; (5) understand the grouping requirements.

学完本课后，你将能够：(1)解释IFRS17的背景和时间线；(2)识别适用范围；(3)描述合同边界概念；(4)解释合同分拆和分解；(5)理解分组要求。

1. Background & Timeline 背景与时间线

中文

IFRS 4（过渡期）：2004年发布，允许各国使用不同方法计量保险负债，缺乏可比性。

IFRS 17（新准则）：2017年5月由IASB正式发布，经过近20年的研究和讨论，取代IFRS 4，是全球统一的保险合同会计准则。

生效日期：2023年1月1日（上市保险公司），2026年1月1日（非上市保险公司）。

CAS 25（中国准则）：财政部2020年12月发布《企业会计准则第25号——保险合同》，与IFRS 17基本趋同。

English

IFRS 4 (Interim Standard): Issued in 2004, allowed entities to use different methods to measure insurance liabilities, resulting in a lack of comparability across entities.

IFRS 17 (New Standard): Officially issued by the IASB in May 2017 after nearly 20 years of research and deliberation. It replaces IFRS 4 and establishes a single global accounting standard for insurance contracts.

Effective Date: January 1, 2023 for listed insurers; January 1, 2026 for non-listed insurers.

CAS 25 (Chinese Standard): China's Ministry of Finance issued CAS 25 in December 2020, substantially converged with IFRS 17.

2. Objective of IFRS 17 IFRS17的目标

IFRS 17 Paragraph 1 — Objective Beginner
The objective of IFRS 17 is to ensure that an entity provides relevant information that faithfully represents those contracts. This information gives a basis for users of financial statements to assess the effect that insurance contracts have on the entity's financial position, financial performance and cash flows.
中文翻译 IFRS 17 的目标是确保主体提供相关信息，真实反映这些合同。该信息为财务报表使用者评估保险合同对主体财务状况、财务业绩和现金流量的影响提供基础。

3. Scope & Definition 适用范围与定义

中文

保险合同：合同的一方（合同签发人）同意在特定的某项不确定的未来事项（保险事项）对合同另一方（保单持有人）产生不利影响时给予其赔偿，从而承担源于保单持有人的重大保险风险。

适用范围：

  • 企业签发的保险合同（含分入再保险）
  • 企业分出的再保险合同
  • 具有相机参与分红特征的投资合同

重大保险风险测试：需评估保险风险是否重大，且保险事项是否对保单持有人产生不利影响。

English

Insurance Contract: A contract under which one party (the issuer) accepts significant insurance risk from another party (the policyholder) by agreeing to compensate the policyholder if a specified uncertain future event (the insured event) adversely affects the policyholder.

Scope:

  • Insurance contracts issued (including inward reinsurance)
  • Outward reinsurance contracts
  • Investment contracts with discretionary participation features (DPF)

Significant Insurance Risk Test: Must assess whether insurance risk is significant and whether the insured event could adversely affect the policyholder.

Key Terms

significant /sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/ 重大的

adversely /ˈædvɜːsli/ 不利地

compensate /ˈkɒmpenseɪt/ 赔偿

scope /skəʊp/ 适用范围

assess /əˈses/ 评估

4. Contract Boundary 合同边界

中文

合同边界确定哪些未来现金流量纳入保险合同组的计量。

企业有权要求保单持有人支付保费或者有实质性义务向保单持有人提供保险合同服务的，该权利或义务所产生的现金流量在合同边界内。

无实质性义务的情形：

  • 企业有实际能力重新评估该保单持有人的风险，并据此可重新设定价格或承诺利益水平以充分反映该风险
  • 企业有实际能力重新评估该合同所属合同组合的风险，并据此可重新设定价格或承诺利益水平
English

The contract boundary determines which future cash flows are included in the measurement of a group of insurance contracts.

Cash flows are within the boundary if the entity has the right to demand premiums from the policyholder or a substantive obligation to provide coverage.

No substantive obligation exists when:

  • The entity has the practical ability to reassess the policyholder's risk and reprice the contract accordingly
  • The entity has the practical ability to reassess the portfolio's risk and reprice accordingly

Key Terms

boundary /ˈbaʊndəri/ 边界

substantive /səbˈstæntɪv/ 实质性的

obligation /ˌɒblɪˈgeɪʃən/ 义务

reassess /ˌriːəˈses/ 重新评估

reprice /riːˈpraɪs/ 重新定价

premium /ˈpriːmiəm/ 保费

5. Separation & Disaggregation 分拆与分解

Key Distinction / 关键区别

Separation (分拆): Distinct components are separated and accounted for under other standards. Disaggregation (分解): Non-distinct investment components are excluded from the income statement but remain part of the insurance liability.

ConceptChineseEnglish DefinitionChinese Definition
Separation 分拆 If a component would be within the scope of another Standard if it were a separate contract, it shall be separated. 如果组成部分是独立的合同（可明确区分的），应当分拆并适用其他准则。
Disaggregation 分解 Non-distinct investment components are excluded from the income statement (insurance service revenue and expense). 不可明确区分的投资部分排除在损益表之外（不影响保险服务收入和费用）。
Investment Component 投资成分 Amounts that must be repaid to the policyholder in all scenarios, regardless of the insured event. 无论保险事项是否发生均须偿还给保单持有人的金额。

6. Grouping of Contracts 合同分组

中文

企业应当将同一合同组合至少分为三组：

  1. 亏损组：初始确认时存在亏损的合同组
  2. 薄利组：初始确认时无显著可能性在未来发生亏损的合同组
  3. 厚利组：该组合中剩余合同组成的合同组

企业不得将签发时间间隔超过一年的合同归入同一合同组。

分组的目的：避免不同获利水平的合同之间交叉补贴，确保亏损及时确认。

English

An entity shall divide a portfolio into at least three groups:

  1. Onerous group: Contracts initially recognized as onerous (loss-making)
  2. Non-onerous group: Contracts with no significant possibility of becoming onerous
  3. Remaining group: The remaining contracts in the portfolio

Contracts issued more than one year apart shall not be included in the same group.

Purpose of grouping: Prevent cross-subssidization between contracts with different profitability levels and ensure timely recognition of losses.

Key Terms

onerous /ˈɒnərəs/ 亏损的

significant /sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/ 显著的

possibility /ˌpɒsəˈbɪlɪti/ 可能性

cross-subsidize /krɒs ˈsʌbsɪdaɪz/ 交叉补贴

profitability /ˌprɒfɪtəˈbɪlɪti/ 获利水平

Memory Tip / 记忆技巧

Think of grouping as "OLR": Onerous → Lucrative (non-onerous) → Remaining. Onerous contracts must be separated first to recognize losses immediately.

分组记忆口诀："亏薄厚"——先分亏损组，再分薄利组，剩余归厚利组。

✍️ Reading Comprehension 阅读理解

Multiple Choice
1. Under IFRS 17, which of the following is NOT required for a contract to be classified as an insurance contract?
  • a) The contract transfers significant insurance risk
  • b) The contract must have a coverage period of at least one year
  • c) An insured event must adversely affect the policyholder
  • d) The issuer agrees to compensate the policyholder
Correct: b) IFRS 17 does not require a minimum coverage period. Short-duration contracts (≤ 1 year) can still be insurance contracts and are measured using PAA.
True or False
2. Contracts issued more than one year apart can be included in the same group if they have similar risks.
  • True
  • False
Correct: False. IFRS 17 explicitly prohibits including contracts issued more than one year apart in the same group, regardless of risk similarity.
Fill in the Blank
3. The _______ determines which future cash flows are included in the measurement of a group of insurance contracts.
  • a) coverage period
  • b) contract boundary
  • c) measurement model
  • d) discount rate
Correct: b) contract boundary. The contract boundary determines which future cash flows are included in the measurement.
