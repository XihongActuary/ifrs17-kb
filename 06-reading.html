Beginner Level 初级 · 核心概念

Passage 1 — Definition of Insurance Contract
Beginner
"The objective of IFRS 17 is to ensure that an entity provides relevant information that faithfully represents those contracts. This information gives a basis for users of financial statements to assess the effect that insurance contracts have on the entity's financial position, financial performance and cash flows."
中文翻译 IFRS 17 的目标是确保实体提供相关信息，以如实反映这些合同。该信息为财务报表使用者评估保险合同对实体的财务状况、财务业绩和现金流量的影响提供基础。
Vocabulary / 词汇 relevant /ˈreləvənt/ adj. 相关的 faithfully represents /ˈfeɪθfəli ˌreprɪˈzents/ 如实反映 assess /əˈses/ v. 评估 financial position /faɪˈnænʃəl pəˈzɪʃən/ 财务状况 financial performance /faɪˈnænʃəl pəˈfɔːməns/ 财务业绩 cash flows /kæʃ fləʊz/ 现金流量
Passage 2 — Fulfilment Cash Flows
Beginner
"An entity shall measure a group of insurance contracts at the initial recognition date at the sum of: (a) the fulfilment cash flows, comprising: (i) estimates of future cash flows; (ii) the discount rate; (iii) the risk adjustment for non-financial risk; and (b) the contractual service margin."
中文翻译 实体应当在初始确认日以如下各项之和对一组保险合同进行计量：(a) 履约现金流，包括：(i) 未来现金流量估计；(ii) 折现率；(iii) 非金融风险的风险调整；以及 (b) 合同服务边际。
Vocabulary / 词汇 fulfilment cash flows /fʊlˈfɪlmənt kæʃ fləʊz/ 履约现金流 discount rate /ˈdɪskaʊnt reɪt/ 折现率 risk adjustment /rɪsk əˈdʒʌstmənt/ 风险调整 initial recognition /ɪˈnɪʃəl ˌrekəɡˈnɪʃən/ 初始确认
Passage 3 — Coverage Period
Beginner
"An entity shall recognise the profit from a group of insurance contracts over the coverage period. The contractual service margin is amortized over the coverage period based on the quantity of coverage units provided."
中文翻译 实体应当在保障期内确认一组保险合同的利润。合同服务边际根据所提供的保障单元数量在保障期内摊销。
Vocabulary / 词汇 recognise /ˈrekəɡnaɪz/ v. 确认 coverage period /ˈkʌvərɪdʒ ˈpɪəriəd/ 保障期 amortize /əˈmɔːtaɪz/ v. 摊销 coverage units /ˈkʌvərɪdʒ ˈjuːnɪts/ 保障单元

Intermediate Level 中级 · 计量规则

Passage 4 — Onerous Contracts
Intermediate
"An insurance contract is onerous at the date of initial recognition if the fulfilment cash flows allocated to the contract, any previously recognised acquisition cash flows and any cash flows arising from the contract at the date of initial recognition in total are a net outflow."
中文翻译 如果分配给该合同的履约现金流、任何先前已确认的获取现金流以及初始确认日源于该合同的现金流合计为净流出，则保险合同在初始确认日即为亏损合同。
Vocabulary / 词汇 onerous /ˈɒnərəs/ adj. 亏损的 net outflow /net ˈaʊtfləʊ/ 净流出 allocated /ˈæləkeɪtɪd/ 分配的 acquisition cash flows /ˌækwɪˈzɪʃən kæʃ fləʊz/ 获取现金流
Passage 5 — Contract Boundary
Intermediate
"An entity shall include in the measurement of a group of insurance contracts all the future cash flows within the boundary of each contract in the group. Cash flows are within the boundary if the entity has a substantive obligation to provide coverage."
中文翻译 实体应当将该组中每份合同边界内的所有未来现金流量纳入一组保险合同的计量。如果实体有提供保障的实质性义务，则该现金流在边界内。
Vocabulary / 词汇 boundary /ˈbaʊndəri/ n. 边界 substantive obligation /səbˈstæntɪv ˌɒblɪˈɡeɪʃən/ 实质性义务 include /ɪnˈkluːd/ v. 纳入
Passage 6 — Grouping
Intermediate
"An entity shall divide a portfolio of insurance contracts into groups. Contracts issued more than one year apart shall not be included in the same group. An entity shall group onerous contracts separately."
中文翻译 实体应当将保险合同组合划分为若干组。发行时间相距超过一年的合同不得纳入同一组。实体应当将亏损合同单独分组。
Vocabulary / 词汇 portfolio /pɔːtˈfəʊliəʊ/ n. 组合 divide /dɪˈvaɪd/ v. 划分 issued /ˈɪʃuːd/ 发行的 separately /ˈsepərətli/ adv. 单独地

Advanced Level 高级 · 复杂计量与列报

Passage 7 — VFA Three Criteria
Advanced
"An insurance contract has direct participation features if: (a) the contractual terms specify that the policyholder participates in a share of a clearly identified pool of underlying items; (b) the entity expects to pay a substantial share of the fair value returns; and (c) a substantial proportion of the variation varies with the fair value of underlying items."
中文翻译 如果满足以下条件，保险合同具有直接参与特征：(a) 合同条款规定保单持有人参与一项清晰可辨认的基础项目池的份额；(b) 实体预期支付公允价值回报的重大份额；以及 (c) 变动的重大比例随基础项目的公允价值而变化。
Vocabulary / 词汇 direct participation /dəˈrekt pɑːˌtɪsɪˈpeɪʃən/ 直接参与 pool /puːl/ n. 池 substantial share /səbˈstænʃəl ʃeə/ 重大份额 variation /ˌveəriˈeɪʃən/ n. 变动
Passage 8 — CSM Absorption
Advanced
"For contracts without direct participation features, the contractual service margin is adjusted for changes in the fulfilment cash flows that relate to future service. Changes arising from financial risk are not adjusted through the CSM."
中文翻译 对于不具有直接参与特征的合同，合同服务边际针对与未来服务相关的履约现金流变动进行调整。因金融风险产生的变动不通过 CSM 调整。
Vocabulary / 词汇 adjusted /əˈdʒʌstɪd/ 调整后的 relate to /rɪˈleɪt tuː/ 与……相关 financial risk /faɪˈnænʃəl rɪsk/ 金融风险 arising from /əˈraɪzɪŋ frɒm/ 源于
Passage 9 — OCI Option
Advanced
"An entity may make an accounting policy choice to present insurance finance income and expenses in a single component of profit or loss, or to split them between profit or loss and other comprehensive income."
中文翻译 实体可以作出会计政策选择，将保险财务收益和费用在利润表单一组成部分中列报，或在利润表与其他综合收益之间拆分列报。
Vocabulary / 词汇 accounting policy choice /əˈkaʊntɪŋ ˈpɒləsi tʃɔɪs/ 会计政策选择 present /prɪˈzent/ v. 列报 component /kəmˈpəʊnənt/ n. 组成部分 split /splɪt/ v. 拆分

Reading Comprehension Questions 阅读理解问答题

Based on the nine passages above. Click an option to reveal the answer. / 基于上述九篇语段。点击选项查看答案。

Multiple Choice / 选择
1. According to Passage 1, what is the objective of IFRS 17?
  • a) To eliminate all insurance contracts from the balance sheet
  • b) To ensure an entity provides relevant information that faithfully represents insurance contracts
  • c) To require insurers to report only cash-based revenue
  • d) To merge insurance service result with finance result into one line
Correct: b). Passage 1 states the objective is "to ensure that an entity provides relevant information that faithfully represents those contracts," giving users a basis to assess the effect of insurance contracts on financial position, financial performance and cash flows.
Multiple Choice / 选择
2. According to Passage 2, which of the following is NOT a component of the fulfilment cash flows at initial recognition?
  • a) Estimates of future cash flows
  • b) The discount rate
  • c) The acquisition cash flows
  • d) The risk adjustment for non-financial risk
Correct: c). Passage 2 lists the fulfilment cash flows as comprising (i) estimates of future cash flows, (ii) the discount rate, and (iii) the risk adjustment for non-financial risk. Acquisition cash flows are mentioned separately in Passage 4 in the context of identifying onerous contracts, not as a component of fulfilment cash flows.
True or False / 判断
3. According to Passage 3, the contractual service margin is amortized based on the quantity of coverage units provided over the coverage period.
  • True
  • False
Correct: True. Passage 3 explicitly states: "The contractual service margin is amortized over the coverage period based on the quantity of coverage units provided." This is the coverage-units approach to CSM release.
Multiple Choice / 选择
4. According to Passages 4 and 6, how should onerous contracts be treated in grouping?
  • a) They must be combined with profitable contracts in the same group
  • b) An entity shall group onerous contracts separately, and a contract is onerous if its fulfilment cash flows plus acquisition cash flows result in a net outflow
  • c) Onerous contracts are excluded from measurement entirely
  • d) Onerous contracts may only be grouped with contracts issued more than one year apart
Correct: b). Passage 6 requires that "an entity shall group onerous contracts separately," and Passage 4 defines a contract as onerous at initial recognition when the allocated fulfilment cash flows, previously recognised acquisition cash flows, and cash flows arising at that date total a net outflow.
Multiple Choice / 选择
5. According to Passage 8, which type of change is not adjusted through the CSM for contracts without direct participation features?
  • a) Changes in fulfilment cash flows that relate to future service
  • b) Changes arising from financial risk
  • c) Changes in the quantity of coverage units provided
  • d) Changes in the contractual service margin at initial recognition
Correct: b). Passage 8 states: "the contractual service margin is adjusted for changes in the fulfilment cash flows that relate to future service. Changes arising from financial risk are not adjusted through the CSM." Financial risk changes flow through profit or loss (or OCI) rather than being absorbed by the CSM.
