1. Income Statement Structure 利润表结构

Learning Objectives / 学习目标

Understand the new IFRS 17 income statement line items, how insurance service revenue is computed (expected vs actual), the exclusion of investment components from revenue, and the decomposition of insurance service result vs insurance finance result. / 理解 IFRS 17 利润表的新列项，保险服务收入的计算逻辑（预期 vs 实际），投资成分从收入费用中剔除，以及保险服务成果与保险财务损益的分解。

中文

IFRS 17 重构了保险公司的利润表，将其拆分为两个独立的部分：

1. 保险服务成果 (Insurance Service Result)
= 保险服务收入 - 保险服务费用

保险服务收入 (Insurance Service Revenue) 的构成：
= 预期赔付 + 预期维持费用 + 风险调整释放 + CSM摊销 + 获客成本摊销

这与旧准则完全不同——旧准则下收入 = 实收保费。新准则下保费不再是收入，而是负债流入。

保险服务费用 (Insurance Service Expense) 的构成：
= 实际发生的赔付 + 获客成本摊销 + 亏损确认/转回 + 其他费用

2. 保险财务损益 (Insurance Finance Income or Expenses)
反映折现率变动和货币时间价值对保险负债的影响，等于"合同负债的利息费用"减去"资产投资的利息收入"的净额。

核心规则：投资成分（Investment Component）——即使不发生保险事故也必须返还给保单持有人的金额——不包含在保险服务收入和保险服务费用中。这确保了利润表只反映保险服务本身的经济实质，而不会将与保险无关的储蓄/返还成分混入经营成果。

English

IFRS 17 restructures the insurer's income statement into two distinct parts:

1. Insurance Service Result
= Insurance Service Revenue - Insurance Service Expense

Insurance Service Revenue consists of:
= Expected claims + Expected maintenance expenses + RA release + CSM amortization + Acquisition cost amortization

This is fundamentally different from the old standard — under the old approach, revenue = premiums received. Under IFRS 17, premiums are no longer revenue; they are a liability inflow.

Insurance Service Expense consists of:
= Actual claims incurred + Acquisition cost amortization + Loss recognition/reversal + Other expenses

2. Insurance Finance Income or Expenses
Reflects the effect of discount rate changes and the time value of money on insurance liabilities. It equals the net of "interest expense on contract liabilities" and "interest income from invested assets."

Key rule: Investment components — amounts that the contract requires the entity to repay to the policyholder even if an insured event does not occur — are excluded from insurance service revenue and insurance service expense. This ensures the income statement reflects only the economic substance of insurance services, without mixing savings/repayment components into operating results.

investment component /ɪnˈvestmənt kəmˈpəʊnənt/ 投资成分

2. Revenue Recognition Logic 收入确认逻辑

中文

旧准则（Old standard）：收入 = 实收保费。保险公司收到保费即记为收入，不考虑保障服务的实际提供节奏。这导致：

  • 时间错配问题 (Time-mismatch)：趸交保费的保单在第一年确认全部收入，但保障服务持续多年，收入与服务的匹配严重扭曲。
  • 投资成分混入问题 (Investment-component mixing)：储蓄型产品中含有的投资返还成分（到期/退保时还给客户的钱）也被计入保费收入，虚增了保险公司经营规模。

IFRS 17（New standard）：收入 = 预期赔付 + 预期维持费用 + CSM 释放 + RA 释放。保费在收到时进入负债（LRC），只有当实体提供了保障服务时，才将对应的 CSM 和预期现金流确认为收入。投资成分始终走资产负债表（负债的增减），不进入利润表的收入/费用。

English

Old standard: Revenue = Premiums received. When insurers receive premiums, they are immediately recorded as revenue, regardless of the timing of coverage service delivery. This causes:

  • Time-mismatch problem: For single-premium policies, all revenue is recognized in year 1, but coverage extends over many years — the matching between revenue and service delivery is severely distorted.
  • Investment-component mixing problem: Savings-type products contain investment repayment components (amounts returned at maturity/surrender to policyholders) that are also counted as premium revenue, inflating the reported operating scale.

IFRS 17 (New standard): Revenue = Expected claims + Expected maintenance expenses + CSM release + RA release. Premiums enter the liability (LRC) upon receipt. Only when the entity has provided coverage services are the corresponding CSM and expected cash flows recognized as revenue. Investment components always flow through the balance sheet (liability increases/decreases), never through P&L revenue/expense.

Example: 5-Year Term Life, 2-Year Premium Payment 案例：5年期定期寿险，2年缴费

中文

某5年期定期寿险产品，客户在保单第1、2年每年缴纳保费 2,000，共 4,000。保单包含投资成分（到期时返还 500），预期每年赔付 800，预期每年维持费用 100，初始 CSM = 600，RA = 200。

旧准则处理：

  • 第1年收入 = 2,000（实收保费），费用 = 赔付支出 + 费用
  • 第3-5年收入 = 0（已无保费进账），但仍在提供服务
  • 投资成分 500 也混在保费收入中 → 虚增收入

IFRS 17 处理：

  • 保费 4,000 在收到时进入 LRC（负债科目），不确认任何收入
  • 每年保险服务收入 = 预期赔付 800 + 预期费用 100 + CSM 摊销 120 + RA 释放 40 = 1,060
  • 5年累计保险服务收入 = 1,060 × 5 = 5,300
  • 投资成分 500 从未进入利润表，仅在负债中滚动

核心差异：旧准则下收入随"保费到账时间"波动，新准则下收入随"服务提供时间"均匀分布。收入与保障服务的匹配度显著提升。

English

A 5-year term life product: the policyholder pays 2,000 per year for the first 2 years, totaling 4,000. The contract includes an investment component (500 returned at maturity). Expected annual claims = 800, expected annual maintenance expenses = 100, initial CSM = 600, initial RA = 200.

Old standard treatment:

  • Year 1 revenue = 2,000 (premium received). Expense = claims + costs.
  • Years 3–5 revenue = 0 (no more premiums), but services are still being provided.
  • The 500 investment component is mixed into premium revenue → inflates reported revenue.

IFRS 17 treatment:

  • The 4,000 premiums enter the LRC (liability) upon receipt. No revenue is recognized at that point.
  • Annual insurance service revenue = expected claims 800 + expenses 100 + CSM amortization 120 + RA release 40 = 1,060
  • 5-year cumulative insurance service revenue = 1,060 × 5 = 5,300
  • The 500 investment component never enters profit or loss — it remains within the liability.

Key difference: Under the old standard, revenue fluctuates with "premium arrival timing." Under IFRS 17, revenue is distributed evenly over the "service delivery period." The matching of revenue to insurance services is dramatically improved.

3. Balance Sheet Structure 资产负债表结构

中文

净值列示 (Net Presentation)：IFRS 17 要求在投资组合层面进行净值列示——不是将所有合同资产和合同负债分别列出，而是按每个投资组合净额在资产负债表中呈现。

保险合同资产/负债 (Insurance Contract Asset/Liability)：
= 保险合同的 FCF（未来 + 过去）+ CSM

当结果为净流出（负债）时列示为"保险合同负债"；当结果为净流入（资产）时列示为"保险合同资产"。

分出再保险合同资产/负债 (Reinsurance Contract Asset/Liability)：
再保分出合同的净值，与直接保险合同分开列示。

负债拆解：
保险合同负债 = LRC (未到期责任负债) + LIC (已发生赔款负债)
LRC = 未来服务的 FCF + CSM
LIC = 过去服务的 FCF (即已发生未决赔款)

这种双层结构清晰地向报表使用者展示了"未来待提供服务对应的负债"和"已发生待赔付对应的负债"的区分。

English

Net Presentation: IFRS 17 requires net presentation at the portfolio level — rather than listing all contract assets and liabilities separately, each portfolio is presented as a net amount on the balance sheet.

Insurance Contract Asset/Liability:
= FCF for insurance contracts (future + past) + CSM

When the result is a net outflow (liability), it is presented as "Insurance Contract Liability." When the result is a net inflow (asset), it is presented as "Insurance Contract Asset."

Reinsurance Contract Asset/Liability:
The net amount of reinsurance contracts held, presented separately from direct insurance contracts.

Liability decomposition:
Insurance Contract Liability = LRC + LIC
LRC = FCF for future service + CSM
LIC = FCF for past service (i.e., incurred but not yet settled claims)

This two-layer structure clearly shows users the distinction between "liabilities for future services to be provided" and "liabilities for past events awaiting settlement."

4. OCI Option OCI选择权

中文

OCI 选择权 (OCI Option) 允许企业在利润表（P&L）和其他综合收益（OCI）之间拆分保险财务损益。

BBA 下的处理：

  • P&L 部分 = 使用 锁定折现率（初始确认时的折现率）计算的"系统性分配"金额
  • OCI 部分 = 当期折现率与锁定折现率差异导致的负债变动
  • 使 P&L 中的财务损益稳定（不受市场利率波动影响），波动部分进入 OCI

VFA 下的处理（镜像匹配）：

  • 实体持有的基础项目（underlying assets）的公允价值变动通过 P&L 或 OCI 列示
  • 保险合同负债对应的财务变动应与基础项目的列示方式"镜像匹配"（mirror matching）
  • 如果基础项目的公允价值变动计入 OCI，则对应的负债财务变动也应计入 OCI
  • 目的：消除因保险合同负债与基础资产会计处理不匹配导致的 P&L 波动

OCI 选择权的意义：
折现率变动对保险负债的影响巨大（尤其是长期业务），若全部计入 P&L，会导致利润表大幅波动，削弱报表使用者对承保经营成果的判断能力。OCI 选择权通过将财务波动"隔离"到其他综合收益中，使 P&L 更清晰地反映保险服务本身的经营成果。

English

OCI Option allows entities to split insurance finance income/expenses between profit or loss (P&L) and other comprehensive income (OCI).

Treatment under BBA:

  • P&L portion = a "systematic allocation" amount calculated using the locked-in discount rate (the rate at initial recognition)
  • OCI portion = the difference in liability movements attributable to changes between current discount rates and locked-in rates
  • This keeps P&L finance income/expenses stable (insulated from market rate volatility), with volatility flowing into OCI

Treatment under VFA (mirror matching):

  • Fair value changes of the underlying assets held by the entity are presented through P&L or OCI
  • The corresponding financial changes in the insurance contract liability should "mirror" (match) the presentation of the underlying assets
  • If the fair value change of underlying items is booked to OCI, then the corresponding liability finance change should also go to OCI
  • Purpose: Eliminate P&L volatility arising from accounting mismatches between insurance liabilities and the underlying assets

Significance of the OCI option:
Discount rate changes have an enormous impact on insurance liabilities (especially for long-duration business). If all were booked to P&L, the income statement would experience severe volatility, undermining users' ability to assess underwriting performance. The OCI option "quarantines" financial volatility into other comprehensive income, allowing P&L to more clearly reflect the operating results of insurance services.

quarantine /ˈkwɒrəntiːn/ 隔离

Practical Note / 实务提示

OCI option is an accounting policy choice made at the entity level for each portfolio. Once elected, it applies consistently. The election must be disclosed in the notes to the financial statements. In practice, many insurers elect the OCI option to reduce P&L volatility, especially for long-tail lines where discount rate sensitivity is high. / OCI选择权是针对每个投资组合在实体层面进行的会计政策选择。一旦选定，需一致适用，并在财务报表附注中披露。实务中，许多保险公司选择使用 OCI 选项以减少 P&L 波动，尤其是在长尾业务中，折现率敏感度较高。

7. Reading Comprehension 阅读理解

Multiple Choice / 选择
1. Under IFRS 17, which of the following is NOT included in insurance service revenue?
  • a) Expected claims for current-period services
  • b) CSM amortization released during the period
  • c) Premiums received from policyholders
  • d) Acquisition cost amortization
Correct: c). Premiums received are NOT included in insurance service revenue under IFRS 17. Instead, premiums enter the LRC upon receipt and are released into revenue as the entity provides coverage services (through CSM amortization, expected claims, and expenses). This is one of the most fundamental differences from the old standard, where premiums were immediately recognized as revenue.
Multiple Choice / 选择
2. What is the purpose of the OCI option for insurance finance income/expenses?
  • a) To allow insurers to avoid recognizing insurance finance expenses entirely
  • b) To reduce P&L volatility from discount rate changes by segregating financial effects into OCI
  • c) To make all insurance liabilities measured at fair value through P&L
  • d) To merge insurance service result with insurance finance result into one line
Correct: b). The OCI option allows entities to split insurance finance income/expenses: a systematic allocation (using locked-in rates) flows through P&L, while the remainder attributable to discount rate changes goes to OCI. This reduces P&L volatility from market interest rate fluctuations and makes underwriting performance more visible to financial statement users.
True or False / 判断
3. Under CAS 25, the balance sheet must present "Insurance Contract Assets" and "Insurance Contract Liabilities" as separate gross line items for each individual contract, rather than as net amounts at the portfolio level.
  • True
  • False
Correct: False. IFRS 17 (and CAS 25) requires net presentation at the portfolio level. Insurance contract assets and liabilities are presented as the net balance of each portfolio — not gross amounts for each individual contract. This means that within a portfolio, contracts in an asset position and contracts in a liability position are netted against each other before presentation on the balance sheet.

8. Discussion Corner 讨论角

Q
Why did IFRS 17 fundamentally change revenue recognition from "premiums received" to "insurance service revenue"? 为什么 IFRS 17 将收入确认从"实收保费"改为"保险服务收入"？
Posted by Learner · 2026

The old standard treated insurance premiums like sales revenue — a cash-inflow-driven approach. This was conceptually flawed for several reasons:

  • Insurance is a service, not a product sale: When a policyholder pays a premium, the insurer has not yet "earned" that money. It has incurred an obligation to provide future coverage. Recognizing revenue at the point of cash receipt misrepresents the economics.
  • Distortion of scale: Long-term savings products bloat reported revenue because investment components (which will be returned to policyholders) are counted as revenue. This makes the insurer appear far larger than its actual insurance service activity.
  • Timing mismatch: Multi-year policies with upfront premiums show all revenue in year 1 and zero in later years, even though coverage persists throughout.

IFRS 17 corrects this by recognizing revenue only as the insurer provides coverage services. This aligns the income statement with the economic reality: insurance is a service, and revenue should reflect the service provided, not the cash collected.

旧准则将保费视为销售收入——这是一种以现金流入驱动的思路，存在本质缺陷。保险是服务而非产品销售，保单持有人缴纳保费时，保险公司并未"赚到"这笔钱，而是承担了未来提供保障的义务。长期储蓄型产品的投资成分虚增了收入规模。IFRS 17 的修正使收入确认与保障服务的提供同步，使利润表与经济实质一致。

Q
What are the practical implications of net presentation for reinsurance contracts? 分出再保险合同净值列示的实务影响是什么？
Posted by Practitioner · 2026

Under IFRS 17, reinsurance contracts held are presented as a separate line item (asset or liability) on the balance sheet, distinct from direct insurance contracts. This has several practical implications:

  • No offsetting: Reinsurance assets cannot be netted against direct insurance liabilities. They must appear as separate balance sheet items. This improves transparency — users can see the gross exposure (direct) and the protection purchased (reinsurance) separately.
  • Reinsurance margins: Similar to CSM for direct contracts, reinsurance contracts held have their own margin (the net cost or net gain on purchasing reinsurance). This is amortized into P&L over the coverage period.
  • Finance impact separation: Reinsurance finance income/expenses are presented separately from direct insurance finance income/expenses, allowing users to see how reinsurance affects the net financial result.

IFRS 17 下，分出再保险合同在资产负债表中单独列示（资产或负债），不与直接保险合同相互抵销。这提高了透明度——报表使用者可以分别看到直接保险的总风险敞口和购买的再保险保护。再保险合同有自己的边际（购买再保险的净成本或净收益），在保障期内摊销计入损益。再保险财务损益也与直接保险财务损益分开列示。

Q
When would an entity elect NOT to use the OCI option? 什么情况下企业不会选择 OCI 选项？
Posted by Analyst · 2026

While the OCI option is popular for reducing P&L volatility, there are scenarios where an entity might elect to recognize all insurance finance income/expenses in P&L:

  • Asset-liability management (ALM) alignment: If the entity's assets are measured at fair value through P&L (FVTPL), then booking all liability finance changes to P&L creates a natural economic offset. Using the OCI option would break this symmetry and could increase total P&L volatility.
  • Short-duration portfolios: For short-tail business (e.g., annual property/casualty), the discounting effect is minimal. The complexity of operating an OCI mechanism may not be justified by the benefit.
  • Simplicity preference: Some entities prefer a simpler accounting framework without the additional OCI tracking, reclassification adjustments, and disclosure requirements that the OCI option entails.
  • VFA considerations: Under VFA, if the entity already uses OCI for underlying assets, mirror matching automatically pushes the corresponding liability change to OCI, making the explicit OCI election less necessary for that portion.

虽然 OCI 选项常用于减少 P&L 波动，但某些场景下企业可能选择将所有保险财务损益计入损益：当资产负债管理（ALM）匹配时，若资产以公允价值计量且变动计入损益（FVTPL），则将所有负债财务变动计入 P&L 形成了自然的经济抵销，使用 OCI 反而可能增大 P&L 波动。短尾业务折现影响很小，复杂的 OCI 机制未必值得维护。部分企业偏好更简洁的会计框架。
