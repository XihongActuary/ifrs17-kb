1. Building Block Approach (BBA) 通用模型 / 要素法

中文

BBA是IFRS17的默认计量模型，适用于不具有直接参与分红特征的保险合同。

保险合同负债 = 履约现金流 (FCF) + 合同服务边际 (CSM)

履约现金流包括三要素：

  1. 预期未来现金流量的估计（无偏、概率加权）
  2. 货币时间价值及金融风险调整（折现率）
  3. 非金融风险调整（RA）

FCF = PVCF + RA，其中 PVCF 包含货币时间价值

English

BBA is the default measurement model under IFRS 17, applicable to insurance contracts without direct participation features.

Insurance Liability = Fulfilment Cash Flows (FCF) + Contractual Service Margin (CSM)

FCF consists of three building blocks:

  1. Estimate of future cash flows (unbiased, probability-weighted)
  2. Discount rate (time value of money and financial risk adjustment)
  3. Risk adjustment for non-financial risk (RA)

FCF = PVCF + RA, where PVCF includes the time value of money

Key Terms

building block /ˈbɪldɪŋ blɒk/ 要素

unbiased /ʌnˈbaɪəst/ 无偏的

probability-weighted /ˌprɒbəˈbɪlɪti weɪtɪd/ 概率加权的

discount rate /ˈdɪskaʊnt reɪt/ 折现率

default /dɪˈfɔːlt/ 默认

BBA Formula / 公式
Insurance Liability = FCF + CSM = (PVCF + RA) + CSM
where: PVCF = Present Value of Future Cash Flows (includes time value of money)
RA = Risk Adjustment for non-financial risk
CSM = Contractual Service Margin (unearned profit, ≥ 0)

2. Variable Fee Approach (VFA) 浮动收费法

中文

VFA适用于具有直接参与分红特征的保险合同，需同时满足三个条件：

  1. 合同条款规定保单持有人参与分享清晰可辨认的基础项目
  2. 企业预计将基础项目公允价值变动回报中的相当大部分支付给保单持有人
  3. 预计应付金额变动中的相当大部分随基础项目公允价值变动而变动

VFA的核心特点：基础项目公允价值变动中主体享有份额的变动通过CSM调整，而非直接计入损益。这实现了资产端和负债端的联动。

English

VFA applies to insurance contracts with direct participation features, which must simultaneously meet three criteria:

  1. Contract terms specify that the policyholder participates in a share of clearly identified underlying items
  2. The entity expects to pay a substantial share of the fair value returns of underlying items to the policyholder
  3. A substantial proportion of the variation in amounts paid varies with the fair value of underlying items

Key feature of VFA: Changes in the entity's share of the fair value of underlying items are adjusted through the CSM rather than recognized directly in profit or loss. This achieves asset-liability linkage.

Key Terms

variable fee /ˈveəriəbl fiː/ 浮动费用

direct participation /daɪˈrekt pɑːtɪsɪˈpeɪʃən/ 直接参与分红

underlying items /ˌʌndəˈlaɪɪŋ ˈaɪtəmz/ 基础项目

fair value /feə ˈvæljuː/ 公允价值

substantial /səbˈstænʃəl/ 相当大的

linkage /ˈlɪŋkɪdʒ/ 联动

VFA Formula / 公式
UI = Underlying Items = VF + FCF
VF = Variable Fee = ES - NU (entity's share minus non-varying FCF)
FCF = UI - ES + NU
CSM absorbs changes in ES (entity's share of underlying items)

3. Premium Allocation Approach (PAA) 保费分配法

中文

PAA是简化计量模型，适用条件：

  1. 合同组内各项合同的责任期不超过一年；或
  2. 企业能够合理预计PAA与BBA计量结果无重大差异

PAA的特点：

  • 初始确认时LRC = 已收保费 - 获取现金流
  • 无需单独计算CSM（简化处理）
  • 保费在责任期内按时间或风险模式分摊为收入
  • 适用于短期险如企财险、家财险、短期健康险等
English

PAA is a simplified measurement model, applicable when:

  1. The coverage period of each contract in the group is one year or less; or
  2. The entity can reasonably expect that the PAA result would not differ materially from the BBA result

Key features of PAA:

  • At initial recognition, LRC = premiums received - acquisition cash flows
  • No separate CSM calculation (simplified)
  • Premiums allocated as revenue over the coverage period
  • Applicable to short-duration products such as property insurance, short-term health insurance

Key Terms

premium allocation /ˈpriːmiəm ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/ 保费分配

materially /məˈtɪəriəli/ 重大地

simplified /ˈsɪmplɪfaɪd/ 简化的

allocate /ˈæləkeɪt/ 分摊

short-duration /ʃɔːt djʊˈreɪʃən/ 短期

✍️ Reading Comprehension

Multiple Choice
1. Which measurement model should be used for a 5-year participating life insurance contract where the policyholder receives a substantial share of the underlying asset pool's returns?
  • a) BBA (Building Block Approach)
  • b) VFA (Variable Fee Approach)
  • c) PAA (Premium Allocation Approach)
  • d) Either BBA or VFA, at entity's choice
Correct: b) VFA. The contract has direct participation features (policyholder shares in underlying items), so VFA is required.
True or False
2. Under PAA, the entity must calculate a separate contractual service margin (CSM).
  • True
  • False
Correct: False. PAA does not require a separate CSM calculation — this is one of its key simplifications.
