企业会计准则第25号——保险合同

Accounting Standard No. 25 — Insurance Contracts (CAS25)
120 Articles 11 Chapters 2023 Effective China
第一章 总则 Chapter 1: General Provisions
Art. 1–6 ▶
本章规定了CAS25的制定目的、保险合同定义、适用范围、排除事项及特殊处理选项。
第一条 Art. 1 制定目的 / Purpose
中文原文

为了规范保险合同的确认、计量和相关信息的列报，根据《企业会计准则——基本准则》，制定本准则。

English Translation

To prescribe the recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure of insurance contracts, this Standard is formulated in accordance with the Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises — Basic Standard.

第二条 Art. 2 保险合同定义 / Definition of Insurance Contract
中文原文

保险合同，是指企业（合同签发人）与保单持有人约定，在特定保险事项对保单持有人产生不利影响时给予其赔偿，从而承担重大保险风险的合同。

English Translation

An insurance contract is a contract under which one party (the issuer) accepts significant insurance risk from another party (the policyholder) by agreeing to compensate the policyholder if a specified uncertain future event (the insured event) adversely affects the policyholder.

第三条 Art. 3 适用范围 / Scope
中文原文

下列各项适用本准则：

（一）企业签发的保险合同（含分入再保险合同）；

（二）企业签发的具有相机参与分红特征的投资合同，但企业同时也是保单持有人的，适用其他相关会计准则；

（三）企业签发的分出再保险合同。

English Translation

This Standard applies to:

(a) Insurance contracts (including reinsurance contracts issued) that the entity issues;

(b) Investment contracts with discretionary participation features that the entity issues, provided that if the entity also holds such contracts, other applicable standards shall apply;

(c) Reinsurance contracts held by the entity.

第四条 Art. 4 排除事项 / Exclusions
中文原文

下列各项适用其他相关会计准则：

（一）企业向保单持有人签发的嵌入在租赁合同中的保险成分；

（二）产品质保，适用《企业会计准则第14号——收入》；

（三）企业合并中取得的或有对价，适用相关准则；

（四）雇主因雇员福利计划形成的资产和负债，适用《企业会计准则第9号——职工薪酬》；

（五）企业发行或持有的具有相机参与分红特征的金融工具，适用《企业会计准则第22号——金融工具确认和计量》；

（六）财务担保合同，除合同签发人明确肯定其为保险合同外；

（七）嵌在其他合同中的保险成分，如信用卡协议中的保险成分。

English Translation

The following shall apply other applicable accounting standards:

(a) Insurance components embedded in lease contracts;

(b) Product warranties — apply CAS14 (Revenue);

(c) Contingent consideration in business combinations;

(d) Employers' assets and liabilities from employee benefit plans — apply CAS9 (Employee Compensation);

(e) Financial instruments with discretionary participation features issued or held by the entity — apply CAS22 (Financial Instruments);

(f) Financial guarantee contracts, unless the issuer explicitly asserts they are insurance contracts;

(g) Insurance components embedded in other contracts, e.g. credit card agreements.

第五条 Art. 5 固定收费服务合同选项 / Fixed-Fee Service Contracts
中文原文

符合保险合同定义但主要目的是为固定收费提供服务的合同，企业可以选择适用本准则或者《企业会计准则第14号——收入》，但应一贯地应用于同类合同。

English Translation

Contracts that meet the definition of an insurance contract but primarily provide services for a fixed fee may be accounted for under either this Standard or CAS14 (Revenue), provided the choice is applied consistently to similar contracts.

第六条 Art. 6 带死亡给付豁免的贷款合同 / Loan Contracts with Death Benefit Waiver
中文原文

符合保险合同定义但对保单持有人的补偿仅限于因死亡而免除偿还贷款余额的合同，企业可以选择适用本准则或者《企业会计准则第22号——金融工具确认和计量》。

English Translation

Contracts that meet the definition of an insurance contract but for which the compensation is limited to the waiver of repayment of an outstanding loan balance on death may be accounted for under either this Standard or CAS22 (Financial Instruments).

第二章 保险合同的识别、合并和分拆 Chapter 2: Identification, Combination and Separation
Art. 7–10 ▶
第七条 Art. 7 重大保险风险评估 / Significant Insurance Risk Assessment
中文原文

企业应当在单项合同层面评估是否转移了重大保险风险。如果合同组内的各合同具有相同的风险特征，企业可以在合同组层面进行评估。

English Translation

An entity shall assess whether an individual contract transfers significant insurance risk. If contracts within a portfolio share the same risk characteristics, the assessment may be performed at the portfolio level.

第八条 Art. 8 合同合并 / Combining Contracts
中文原文

企业应当将以实现整体商业目的而订立的一系列合同，或者合同权利或义务关联的一系列合同，合并为一项合同处理。

English Translation

An entity shall combine a portfolio of insurance contracts if entered into with the same or related counterparty to achieve an overall commercial effect, or if the rights or obligations are interrelated.

第九条 Art. 9 分拆处理 / Separation of Components
中文原文

保险合同包含一个或多个组成部分的，企业应当从保险合同中分拆并对以下组成部分单独进行会计处理：

（一）符合《企业会计准则第22号》规定的嵌入衍生工具；

（二）与保险合同不高度关联的投资成分；

（三）向保单持有人转让的商品或非保险服务的承诺。

English Translation

An entity shall separate the following components from an insurance contract and account for them separately:

(a) Embedded derivatives meeting the criteria in CAS22 (Financial Instruments);

(b) Investment components that are not highly interrelated with the insurance components;

(c) Promises to transfer goods or non-insurance services to the policyholder.

第十条 Art. 10 分拆后的现金流量分配 / Cash Flow Allocation after Separation
中文原文

企业分拆保险合同组成部分后，应当将保险合同边界的现金流量与分拆的组成部分的现金流量进行区分。不能直接归属的现金流量，应当采用合理的基础进行分配。

English Translation

After separating components, an entity shall distinguish cash flows within the insurance contract boundary from those of separated components. Cash flows not directly attributable shall be allocated on a reasonable basis.

第三章 保险合同的分组 Chapter 3: Grouping of Insurance Contracts
Art. 11–15 ▶
第十一条 Art. 11 合同组合 / Portfolio
中文原文

企业应当将具有相似风险且统一管理的保险合同归为同一保险合同组合。

English Translation

An entity shall combine insurance contracts with similar risks and managed together into a portfolio.

第十二条 Art. 12 最小组别 / Minimum Groups
中文原文

企业应当将保险合同组合在初始确认时至少划分为下列合同组：

（一）亏损合同组；

（二）初始确认时无显著可能性转变为亏损合同组的其他合同；

（三）该组合中的剩余合同。

English Translation

An entity shall divide a portfolio into a minimum of the following groups at initial recognition:

(a) Onerous groups;

(b) Contracts with no significant possibility of becoming onerous subsequently;

(c) Remaining contracts in the portfolio.

第十三条 Art. 13 进一步细分 / Further Subdivision
中文原文

企业可以根据需要在上述分组基础上对合同组进行进一步细分，但不得将签发时间间隔超过一年的合同归入同一合同组。

English Translation

An entity may further subdivide groups as needed, but shall not include contracts issued more than one year apart in the same group.

第十四条 Art. 14 评估基础 / Assessment Basis
中文原文

企业应当在合同组初始确认时评估该组是否属于亏损合同组，评估应当基于合理的和可支持的信息进行。

English Translation

An entity shall assess at initial recognition whether a group is onerous, based on reasonable and supportable information.

第十五条 Art. 15 监管约束 / Regulatory Constraints
中文原文

法律法规限制企业将具有不同盈利特征的合同分开管理的，企业可以将该等合同归入同一合同组。

English Translation

Where law or regulation restricts the entity from managing contracts with different profitability characteristics separately, such contracts may be grouped together.

第四章 确认 Chapter 4: Recognition
Art. 16–19 ▶
第十六条 Art. 16 确认时点 / Recognition Timing
中文原文

企业应当自下列时点中的最早时点起确认其签发的保险合同组：

（一）保障责任期间开始日；

（二）保单持有人首次付款到期日；

（三）亏损合同组发生亏损时。

English Translation

An entity shall recognise a group of insurance contracts from the earliest of:

(a) The beginning of the coverage period;

(b) The due date of the first payment from the policyholder;

(c) When facts indicate the group is onerous.

第十七条 Art. 17 保险获取现金流量资产 / Acquisition Cash Flow Asset
中文原文

企业在合同组确认前已支付的保险获取现金流量，应当确认为资产。在合同组确认时，企业应当终止确认该资产并将其包含在该合同组的计量中。

English Translation

Insurance acquisition cash flows paid before the group is recognised shall be recognised as an asset. Upon group recognition, the entity shall derecognise the asset and include it in the group's measurement.

第十八条 Art. 18 获取资产终止确认 / Derecognition of Acquisition Asset
中文原文

在合同组确认时，企业应当将此前确认为资产的相关保险获取现金流量终止确认，并计入合同组的计量。

English Translation

Upon recognition of a group, the entity shall derecognise the previously recognised acquisition cash flow asset and include it in the measurement of the group.

第十九条 Art. 19 减值 / Impairment
中文原文

企业应当在每个报告日，评估保险合同获取现金流量资产的减值迹象，已发生减值的应当确认减值损失。

English Translation

At each reporting date, an entity shall assess the insurance acquisition cash flow asset for impairment indicators and recognise an impairment loss if applicable.

第五章 计量 — 第一节 一般规定 Chapter 5: Measurement — Section 1: General Requirements (BBA)
Art. 20–38 ▶
第二十条 Art. 20 计量单位与初始计量公式 / Measurement Unit & Initial Formula
中文原文

企业应当以合同组作为计量单元。初始确认时，合同组的计量金额为履约现金流量与合同服务边际之和。

English Translation

An entity shall measure a group at initial recognition as the sum of the fulfilment cash flows and the contractual service margin.

第二十一条 Art. 21 履约现金流量组成 / FCF Components
中文原文

履约现金流量包括下列各项：

（一）与履行合同直接相关的未来现金流量估计数；

（二）反映货币时间价值和与未来现金流量相关金融风险的折现率；

（三）对非金融风险的风险调整。

English Translation

The fulfilment cash flows comprise:

(a) Estimates of future cash flows directly related to fulfilling the contracts;

(b) The discount rate reflecting the time value of money and financial risks;

(c) The risk adjustment for non-financial risk.

第二十三条 Art. 23 未来现金流量估计要求 / Future CF Estimation
中文原文

企业估计未来现金流量应当：

（一）采用无偏的、概率加权的预期值；

（二）反映企业能够获取的所有合理和可支持的信息；

（三）反映可观察到的当前市场变量；

（四）不包括自身的信用风险变化。

English Translation

An entity shall estimate future cash flows:

(a) Using unbiased, probability-weighted estimates;

(b) Reflecting all reasonable and supportable information available;

(c) Reflecting observable current market variables;

(d) Excluding changes in own credit risk.

第二十四条 Art. 24 合同边界 / Contract Boundary
中文原文

企业有权要求保单持有人支付保费或者有实质性义务向保单持有人提供服务的期间内的现金流量，属于保险合同边界内的现金流量。

English Translation

An entity shall include all future cash flows within the boundary of each contract — the period during which the entity has the right to require the policyholder to pay premiums or a substantive obligation to provide services.

第二十五条 Art. 25 折现率 / Discount Rate
中文原文

折现率应当反映货币时间价值、现金流量特征以及保险合同的流动性特征。企业应当采用与保险合同现金流量特征一致的折现率，且不得因持有相关资产而调整折现率。

English Translation

The discount rate shall reflect the time value of money, the characteristics of the cash flows and the liquidity characteristics of the insurance contracts. Rates shall be consistent with observable current market prices and shall not be adjusted for assets held.

第二十六条 Art. 26 非金融风险调整 / Risk Adjustment for Non-Financial Risk
中文原文

企业应当包括对非金融风险的风险调整，反映企业因承担非金融风险所要求的补偿金额。

English Translation

An entity shall include a risk adjustment for non-financial risk reflecting the compensation the entity requires for bearing uncertainty about the amount and timing of cash flows from non-financial risk.

第二十七条 Art. 27 初始CSM计算 / Initial CSM Calculation
中文原文

企业应当在合同组初始确认时计算下列各项之和：

（一）履约现金流量；

（二）在该日终止确认保险获取现金流量资产以及其他相关资产或负债对应的现金流量；

（三）合同组内合同在该日产生的现金流量。

上述各项之和反映为现金净流入的，企业应当将其确认为合同服务边际；反映为现金净流出的，企业应当将其作为首日亏损计入当期损益。

English Translation

An entity shall calculate the sum of the following at initial recognition of a group:

(a) The fulfilment cash flows;

(b) Cash flows from derecognition of insurance acquisition cash flow assets and other related assets or liabilities; and

(c) Cash flows arising from contracts in the group at that date.

If the sum results in a net inflow, the entity shall recognise it as the contractual service margin; if a net outflow, as a day-1 loss in profit or loss.

第二十八条 Art. 28 后续计量 / Subsequent Measurement
中文原文

企业应当在后续计量日按照未到期责任负债与已发生赔款负债之和计量合同组。

English Translation

An entity shall measure the group at subsequent measurement dates as the sum of the liability for remaining coverage and the liability for incurred claims.

第二十九条 Art. 29 CSM滚动变动（非DPF） / CSM Roll-Forward (Non-DPF)
中文原文

不具有直接参与分红特征的合同组的合同服务边际，应当在每个报告日按照下列各项进行调整：

（一）因新合同加入合同组而增加的部分；

（二）因货币时间价值和金融风险的影响产生的利息；

（三）因与未来服务相关的履约现金流量变化导致的部分；

（四）因外汇汇兑差异影响的部分；

（五）因当期按照保障责任单元摊销计入保险服务收入的部分。

English Translation

For groups without direct participation features, the contractual service margin shall be adjusted at each reporting date for:

(a) Increases from new contracts added to the group;

(b) Interest from the effect of the time value of money and financial risk;

(c) Changes in fulfilment cash flows relating to future service;

(d) Foreign exchange differences;

(e) Amortisation recognised as insurance service revenue based on coverage units.

第三十条 Art. 30 保障责任单元摊销 / Coverage Unit Amortization
中文原文

企业应当确定保障责任单元，并按照这些单元的消耗情况将合同服务边际摊销计入当期损益。

English Translation

An entity shall determine coverage units and amortise the contractual service margin to profit or loss based on the consumption of those units.

第三十一条 Art. 31 收入与费用确认 / Revenue and Expense Recognition
中文原文

企业应当将未到期责任负债的减少确认为保险服务收入，包括当期预期赔付、风险调整释放、CSM摊销以及获取费用摊销等。

English Translation

An entity shall recognise the decrease in the liability for remaining coverage as insurance service revenue, including expected claims, release of risk adjustment, CSM amortisation and acquisition cost amortisation.

第三十三条 Art. 33 保险合同金融变动额 / Insurance Finance Income/Expenses (IFIE)
中文原文

保险合同金融变动额，是指因货币时间价值和金融风险影响导致的保险合同负债账面价值变动。

English Translation

IFRS17 finance income or expenses (IFIE) refers to changes in the carrying amount of insurance contract liabilities arising from the effect of the time value of money and financial risk.

第三十四条 Art. 34 OCI选择权 / OCI Option
中文原文

企业可以选择将所有保险合同金融变动额计入当期损益，或将其中部分计入其他综合收益。

English Translation

An entity may make an accounting policy choice to present all IFIE in profit or loss, or to split a portion to other comprehensive income.

第五章 第二节 具有直接参与分红特征的合同 Ch.5 §2: Contracts with Direct Participation Features (VFA)
Art. 39–45 ▶
第四十条 Art. 40 直接参与分红特征定义 / DPF Definition
中文原文

同时满足下列条件的保险合同，属于具有直接参与分红特征的保险合同：

（一）合同条款规定保单持有人参与清晰可辨认的基础项目池的份额；

（二）企业预期将基础项目公允价值回报的重大份额支付给保单持有人；

（三）企业预期支付给保单持有人的金额变动中，重大比例随基础项目公允价值变动而变动。

English Translation

A contract has direct participation features if all of the following apply:

(a) The contractual terms specify that the policyholder participates in a share of a clearly identified pool of underlying items;

(b) The entity expects to pay the policyholder a substantial share of the fair value returns on the underlying items;

(c) A substantial proportion of the expected changes in amounts payable varies with changes in the fair value of underlying items.

第四十一条 Art. 41 浮动收费法 / Variable Fee Approach
中文原文

具有直接参与分红特征的合同组，应当按照浮动收费法进行计量。该法下，企业应将其在基础项目公允价值中的份额减去履约现金流量后的金额确认为合同服务边际。

English Translation

Groups of contracts with direct participation features shall be measured using the variable fee approach (VFA). The entity shall recognise its share of the fair value of underlying items less fulfilment cash flows as the contractual service margin.

第四十二条 Art. 42 DPF下CSM滚动变动 / CSM Roll-Forward for DPF
中文原文

具有直接参与分红特征的合同组的合同服务边际调整包括：

（一）新合同加入的影响；

（二）企业所享有的基础项目公允价值份额的变动（不含CSM锁定的利率计息）；

（三）与未来服务相关的履约现金流量变动；

（四）外汇汇兑差异；

（五）当期摊销计入保险服务收入的金额。

English Translation

For DPF groups, the contractual service margin is adjusted for:

(a) New contracts added to the group;

(b) The entity's share of changes in the fair value of underlying items (excluding amounts locked in CSM at the discount rate);

(c) Changes in fulfilment cash flows relating to future service;

(d) Foreign exchange differences;

(e) Amortisation recognised as insurance service revenue.

第四十三条 Art. 43 风险管理缓释选项 / Risk Management Mitigation
中文原文

企业持有衍生工具或使用分出的再保险来缓释金融风险，且满足特定条件的，可以将该等风险缓释影响纳入合同服务边际或损益。

English Translation

If an entity uses derivatives or reinsurance held to mitigate financial risk and certain conditions are met, the entity may include the mitigation effect in the CSM or profit or loss.

第四十四条 Art. 44 DPF持有人的OCI选择 / OCI Option for DPF
中文原文

具有直接参与分红特征的合同组，企业可以选择将保险合同金融变动额计入当期损益和其他综合收益之间进行分摊。

English Translation

For DPF groups, an entity may choose to disaggregate IFIE between profit or loss and other comprehensive income.

第四十五条 Art. 45 再保险排除 / Reinsurance Exclusion
中文原文

第二节的规定不适用于分出再保险合同。

English Translation

Section 2 does not apply to reinsurance contracts held.

第五章 第三节 亏损合同组 Ch.5 §3: Onerous Groups
Art. 46–49 ▶
第四十六条 Art. 46 首日亏损及后续亏损 / Day-1 and Subsequent Loss
中文原文

初始确认时属于亏损合同组的，企业应当将净现金流出确认为首日亏损计入损益。后续期间发生亏损的，应当将追加损失计入当期损益并增加未到期责任负债的亏损成分。

English Translation

For onerous groups at initial recognition, the net cash outflow is recognised as a day-1 loss in profit or loss. Subsequent losses shall be recognised in profit or loss and increase the loss component of the liability for remaining coverage.

第四十七条 Art. 47 后续亏损触发条件 / Subsequent Loss Triggers
中文原文

企业应当在每个报告日评估是否存在以下导致合同组变为亏损的情形：

（一）与未来服务相关的履约现金流量的不利变动超过合同服务边际的剩余账面价值；

（二）具有直接参与分红特征的合同组，企业享有的基础项目份额的不利变动超过合同服务边际。

English Translation

At each reporting date, an entity shall assess whether:

(a) Adverse changes in fulfilment cash flows relating to future service exceed the remaining CSM; or

(b) For DPF groups, adverse changes in the entity's share of underlying items exceed the CSM.

第四十八条 Art. 48 亏损成分分配 / Loss Component Allocation
中文原文

企业应当系统地将未到期责任负债的亏损成分与各期保险服务收入和保险服务费用的确认进行匹配。

English Translation

An entity shall systematically allocate the loss component of the liability for remaining coverage with the recognition of insurance service revenue and insurance service expenses each period.

第四十九条 Art. 49 亏损转回 / Loss Reversal
中文原文

后续期间合同组亏损金额减少的，企业应当转回已确认的亏损并计入当期损益，但转回金额不得超过已确认的累计亏损。

English Translation

If the loss on a group reduces in subsequent periods, the entity shall reverse the recognised loss up to the cumulative amount previously recognised in profit or loss.

第五章 第四节 简化处理（保费分配法）Ch.5 §4: Simplified Approach (PAA)
Art. 50–57 ▶
第五十条 Art. 50 保费分配法适用条件 / PAA Eligibility
中文原文

企业可以对满足下列条件之一的保险合同组采用保费分配法：

（一）企业合理预期采用保费分配法下合同组的未到期责任负债计量结果与一般模型无重大差异；

（二）合同组内每项合同的保障责任期间不超过一年。

English Translation

An entity may use the premium allocation approach (PAA) if either:

(a) The entity reasonably expects that measuring the LRC under PAA would not differ materially from the general model; or

(b) The coverage period of each contract in the group is no more than one year.

第五十三条 Art. 53 PAA未到期责任负债计算 / PAA LRC Formula
中文原文

企业初始确认时，未到期责任负债为收到的保费减去保险获取现金流量（若企业选择不将其确认为资产）。后续调整包括已赚保费摊销、亏损部分的确认以及相关金融变动。

English Translation

On initial recognition, the LRC is the premiums received less acquisition cash flows (if not recognised as an asset). Subsequently adjusted for earned premium amortisation, onerous loss recognition, and related financial changes.

第五十五条 Art. 55 PAA下的亏损确认 / Loss Recognition under PAA
中文原文

若合同组在后续期间发生亏损，企业应当在采用保费分配法时单独确认亏损成分。

English Translation

If a group becomes onerous under PAA, the entity shall separately recognise a loss component.

第五十七条 Art. 57 PAA下的收入确认 / Revenue under PAA
中文原文

采用保费分配法的企业应当按照时间流逝或其他合理基础确认保险服务收入。

English Translation

Under PAA, the entity shall recognise insurance service revenue based on the passage of time or another reasonable basis.

第六章 分出再保险合同 Chapter 6: Reinsurance Contracts Held
Art. 58–72 ▶
第五十八条 Art. 58 一般办法 / General Approach
中文原文

企业应当将持有的分出再保险合同作为独立的合同组进行确认和计量，适用一般模型，但需按本章进行调整。

English Translation

An entity shall recognise and measure reinsurance contracts held as separate groups using the general model, with adjustments as specified in this chapter.

第六十二条 Art. 62 再保险资产初始计量 / Reinsurance Asset Initial Measurement
中文原文

企业应当在分出再保险合同组初始确认时，将其计量为履约现金流量与代表分出再保险合同组净成本或净收益的合同服务边际之和。

English Translation

An entity shall measure a group of reinsurance contracts held at initial recognition as the sum of the fulfilment cash flows and the CSM representing the net cost or net gain from the reinsurance.

第六十五条 Art. 65 再保险CSM：净成本或净收益 / CSM for Reinsurance: Net Cost or Net Gain
中文原文

分出再保险合同组初始确认时，履约现金流量与分出保费差额形成净成本的，企业应当将其确认为合同服务边际；差额形成净收益的，确认为合同服务边际负值。

English Translation

At initial recognition, if the difference between fulfilment cash flows and ceded premiums represents a net cost, the entity shall recognise it as CSM; if a net gain, as negative CSM.

第六十八条 Art. 68 再保险CSM滚动变动 / CSM Roll-Forward for Reinsurance
中文原文

分出再保险合同组的合同服务边际调整包括：

（一）新合同加入的影响；

（二）货币时间价值与金融风险利息；

（三）未来服务相关履约现金流量变动（不含标的保险合同亏损转回的影响）；

（四）标的保险合同亏损确认对再保险CSM的调整；

（五）外汇汇兑差异；

（六）当期摊销计入损益的金额。

English Translation

The CSM for reinsurance contracts held is adjusted for:

(a) Effect of new contracts added;

(b) Interest on the time value of money and financial risk;

(c) Changes in FCF relating to future service (excluding reversal of losses on underlying contracts);

(d) Adjustment for losses recognised on underlying contracts;

(e) Foreign exchange differences;

(f) Amounts amortised to profit or loss.

第七章 合同转让或被合并 Chapter 7: Transfer or Business Combination
Art. 73–76 ▶
第七十四条 Art. 74 视同新合同处理 / Treat as New Contract
中文原文

企业通过合同转让或企业合并取得的保险合同，应当视为在取得日订立的新合同进行会计处理。

English Translation

An entity shall treat acquired insurance contracts as if they were entered into at the acquisition date.

第八章 修改和终止确认 Chapter 8: Modification and Derecognition
Art. 77–82 ▶
第七十七条 Art. 77 重大修改标准 / Substantial Modification Criteria
中文原文

保险合同条款发生重大修改的，企业应当终止确认原合同并将修改后的合同作为新合同进行确认。重大修改的判断应基于对合同现金流量或条款的定性或定量评估。

English Translation

If the terms of an insurance contract are substantially modified, the entity shall derecognise the original contract and recognise the modified contract as a new contract. Assessment shall be qualitative or quantitative based on contractual cash flows and terms.

第七十八条 Art. 78 终止确认条件 / Derecognition
中文原文

企业应当在保险合同的权利义务被解除、撤销、取消或到期时终止确认该合同。

English Translation

An entity shall derecognise an insurance contract when the rights and obligations are extinguished, discharged, cancelled or expire.

第九章 列报 Chapter 9: Presentation
Art. 83–105 ▶
第八十四条 Art. 84 资产负债表项目 / Balance Sheet Items
中文原文

企业应当在资产负债表中单独列示以下项目：

（一）保险合同负债；

（二）保险合同资产；

（三）分出再保险合同资产；

（四）分出再保险合同负债。

English Translation

An entity shall present separately in the statement of financial position:

(a) Insurance contract liabilities;

(b) Insurance contract assets;

(c) Reinsurance contract assets;

(d) Reinsurance contract liabilities.

第八十五条 Art. 85 利润表项目 / Income Statement Items
中文原文

企业应当在利润表中列示以下项目：

（一）保险服务收入；

（二）保险服务费用；

（三）分出再保险的分摊保费；

（四）分出再保险的摊回赔款和摊回费用；

（五）承保财务损益；

（六）分出再保险财务损益。

English Translation

An entity shall present in the statement of profit or loss:

(a) Insurance service revenue;

(b) Insurance service expenses;

(c) Reinsurance premium allocation;

(d) Reinsurance recoveries;

(e) Insurance finance result;

(f) Reinsurance finance result.

第八十六条 Art. 86 调节表 / Reconciliation Tables
中文原文

企业应当在附注中披露各合同组净账面价值的期初至期末调节表，包括合同服务边际、风险调整、亏损成分、履约现金流量等变动分析。

English Translation

An entity shall disclose reconciliations from opening to closing net carrying amounts for each group, including analysis of CSM, risk adjustment, loss component, and fulfilment cash flow movements.

第八十八条–第一百零五条 Art. 88–105 其他披露要求 / Additional Disclosure Requirements
中文原文

关键披露要求摘要：

（一）如何区分保险合同组合及合同组的依据；

（二）确认和计量方法、重要判断及其变化；

（三）折现率、风险调整等重大假设及敏感性分析；

（四）新业务CSM本期增加额及预期未来摊销模式；

（五）过渡金额说明。

English Translation

Key disclosure requirements summary:

(a) Basis for distinguishing portfolios and groups;

(b) Recognition and measurement methods, significant judgments and changes;

(c) Discount rates, risk adjustments and sensitivity analysis;

(d) New business CSM and expected amortisation pattern;

(e) Transition amount explanations.

第十章 衔接规定 Chapter 10: Transition
Art. 106–119 ▶
第一百零六条 Art. 106 追溯调整法 / Retrospective Approach
中文原文

企业应当对首次执行日前已签发的保险合同组采用追溯调整法，除非该法不切实可行。

English Translation

An entity shall apply the retrospective approach to groups of insurance contracts issued before the date of initial application, unless impracticable.

第一百零八条 Art. 108 修正追溯法与公允价值法 / Modified Retrospective and Fair Value
中文原文

追溯调整法不切实可行的，企业可以采用修正追溯调整法或公允价值法。两种方法应当一贯应用且进行适当披露。

English Translation

If full retrospective application is impracticable, the entity may apply the modified retrospective approach or the fair value approach. The chosen method shall be applied consistently with appropriate disclosure.

第一百一十条 Art. 110 非DPF修正追溯法 / Modified Retrospective for Non-DPF
中文原文

对于不具有直接参与分红特征的合同组，修正追溯调整法下应当使用合理且可支持的信息确定过渡日CSM或亏损成分。

English Translation

For non-DPF groups under the modified retrospective approach, the entity shall determine the CSM or loss component at the transition date using reasonable and supportable information.

第一百一十五条 Art. 115 公允价值法 / Fair Value Approach
中文原文

公允价值法下，企业应当确定过渡日合同组的公允价值，并将CSM确定为过渡日公允价值与履约现金流量（可能包含调整）之间的差额。

English Translation

Under the fair value approach, the entity shall determine the fair value of the group at the transition date, with CSM being the difference between fair value and fulfilment cash flows (possibly adjusted).

第十一章 附则 Chapter 11: Supplementary Provisions
Art. 120 ▶
第一百二十条 Art. 120 生效日期 / Effective Date
中文原文

本准则自2023年1月1日起施行。企业应当自2023年1月1日起执行本准则，并按照衔接规定进行处理。

English Translation

This Standard becomes effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023. An entity shall apply this Standard from 1 January 2023 and follow the transition provisions.
