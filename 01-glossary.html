Accounting Standards / 会计准则

IFRS 17 IFRS17 /ˌaɪ ef ɑːr es ˈsevəntiːn/
国际财务报告准则第17号——保险合同
An international financial reporting standard that establishes the accounting and reporting requirements for insurance contracts, issued by the IASB in May 2017, effective from January 1, 2023.
由国际会计准则理事会（IASB）于2017年5月发布的国际财务报告准则，规范保险合同的确认、计量和列报要求，自2023年1月1日起生效。
CAS 25 CAS25 /siː eɪ es twenti faɪv/
企业会计准则第25号——保险合同
Chinese Accounting Standard No. 25 — Insurance Contracts, issued by the Ministry of Finance in December 2020, largely converged with IFRS 17, effective from January 1, 2023 (listed) and January 1, 2026 (others).
财政部2020年12月发布的保险合同会计准则，与IFRS17基本趋同，自2023年1月1日（上市）和2026年1月1日（非上市）起施行。
International Accounting Standards Board IASB /ˌɪntəˈnæʃənl əˈkaʊntɪŋ ˈstændədz bɔːd/
国际会计准则理事会
The independent standard-setting body of the IFRS Foundation responsible for developing and approving international financial reporting standards (IFRSs).
IFRS基金会的独立准则制定机构，负责制定和批准国际财务报告准则。
IFRS 9 IFRS9 /ˌaɪ ef ɑːr es naɪn/
国际财务报告准则第9号——金融工具
International standard for financial instruments classification and measurement. Works alongside IFRS 17 — insurers must align asset classification (AC, FVOCI, FVPL) with insurance liability measurement models.
金融工具分类计量的国际准则，与IFRS17配合使用。保险公司需将资产分类（摊余成本、FVOCI、FVPL）与保险负债计量模型匹配。
Other Comprehensive Income OCI /ˈʌðə ˌkɒmprɪˈhensɪv ˈɪnkʌm/
其他综合收益
Gains and losses that are excluded from net income but included in comprehensive income. Under IFRS 17, entities can elect to route insurance finance income/expenses through OCI to reduce volatility.
不计入当期损益但计入综合收益的利得和损失。IFRS17下，主体可选择将保险财务收益/费用计入OCI以减少利润波动。
The OCI option allows insurers to match the timing of insurance finance expenses with investment income recognized in profit or loss.
Insurance Finance Income or Expenses IFIE /ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈfaɪnæns ˈɪnkʌm ɔːr ɪkˈspensɪz/
保险财务收益或费用
The change in the carrying amount of insurance liabilities arising from the passage of time (interest accretion) and changes in financial assumptions (e.g., discount rate changes).
保险合同负债因时间流逝（计息）和金融假设变动（如折现率变化）而产生的账面价值变动。
Full Retrospective Approach /fʊl ˌretrəˈspektɪv əˈprəʊtʃ/
完全追溯法
A transition method that applies IFRS 17 as if it had always been applied, restating prior periods.
过渡方法之一，假设自合同初始确认起即按IFRS17处理，追溯调整前期数据。
Modified Retrospective Approach /ˈmɒdɪfaɪd ˌretrəˈspektɪv əˈprəʊtʃ/
修正追溯法
A transition method used when full retrospective application is not practicable, using reasonable and reliable information available at the transition date to approximate full retrospective results.
完全追溯法不切实可行时采用的过渡方法，利用过渡日可获得的合理可靠信息，以接近追溯法结果为目标进行简化处理。
Fair Value Approach /feə ˈvæljuː əˈprəʊtʃ/
公允价值法
A transition method where the CSM at transition date is determined as the difference between the fair value of the group of contracts and the fulfilment cash flows.
过渡方法之一，以过渡日合同组公允价值与履约现金流量的差额确定合同服务边际。
Transition Date /trænˈzɪʃən deɪt/
过渡日
The beginning of the earliest reporting period for which comparative information is presented under IFRS 17.
列报经调整的更早期间比较信息的最早期间的期初。

Insurance Contracts / 保险合同

Insurance Contract /ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈkɒntrækt/
保险合同
A contract under which one party (the issuer) accepts significant insurance risk from another party (the policyholder) by agreeing to compensate the policyholder if a specified uncertain future event (the insured event) adversely affects the policyholder.
合同的一方（合同签发人）同意在特定的某项不确定的未来事项（保险事项）对合同另一方（保单持有人）产生不利影响时给予其赔偿，从而承担源于保单持有人的重大保险风险。
A term life insurance policy is an insurance contract because the insurer accepts the risk of the policyholder's death.
Policyholder /ˈpɒlɪsiˌhəʊldə/
保单持有人
A party that has a right to compensation under an existing insurance contract if an insured event occurs.
按照现有保险合同，在保险事项发生时有权获得赔偿的合同一方。
Insured Event /ɪnˈʃʊəd ɪˈvent/
保险事项
A specified uncertain future event that is covered by an insurance contract and gives rise to insurance risk.
保险合同所承保的、产生保险风险的不确定的未来事项。
Insurance Risk /ɪnˈʃʊərəns rɪsk/
保险风险
Risk, other than financial risk, transferred from the policyholder to the issuer. Insurance risk is risk that is not a financial risk.
从合同持有人转移至合同签发人的除金融风险之外的风险。
Financial Risk /faɪˈnænʃəl rɪsk/
金融风险
The risk of a possible future change in one or more specified interest rates, financial instrument prices, commodity prices, exchange rates, price or rate indices, credit ratings or credit indices, or other variables.
一项或多项特定利率、金融工具价格、商品价格、汇率、物价或利率指数、信用等级或信用指数或者其他变量在未来可能发生变化的风险。
Coverage Period CP /ˈkʌvərɪdʒ ˈpɪəriəd/
保险责任期
The period during which the entity provides coverage for insured events. This period includes coverage relating to all premiums within the boundary of the insurance contract.
主体为保险事项提供保障的期间。该期间包括与保险合同边界内所有保费有关的保障。
Investment Component IC /ɪnˈvestmənt kəmˈpəʊnənt/
投资成分
The amounts that an insurance contract requires the entity to repay to the policyholder in all scenarios, regardless of whether the insured event occurs.
在所有情况下，无论保险事项是否发生，保险合同要求主体偿还给保单持有人的金额。
The account value in a universal life policy is an investment component.
Separation (of components) /ˌsepəˈreɪʃən/
分拆
The process of identifying and separating distinct components of an insurance contract (e.g., embedded derivatives, distinct investment components, distinct goods or services) that would be within the scope of another Standard if they were separate contracts.
一项保险合同可能含有一个或多个组成部分，这些组成部分如果是独立的合同（可明确区分的），则可能属于另一准则的适用范围，应当分拆。
Disaggregation /dɪsˈægrɪgeɪʃən/
分解
The process of excluding non-distinct investment components from the income statement. Disaggregation differs from separation — the non-distinct investment component does not constitute a separate accounting unit.
IFRS17将不可明确区分的投资部分排除在损益表之外（即分解）。分解不同于分拆，不可明确区分的投资部分本身不构成最小会计核算单元，只是其一部分。
Contract Boundary /ˈkɒntrækt ˈbaʊndəri/
合同边界
The boundary that determines which future cash flows are included in the measurement of a group of insurance contracts. Cash flows are within the boundary if the entity has a substantive obligation to provide coverage or the right to demand premiums.
确定哪些未来现金流量纳入保险合同组计量的边界。企业有权要求保单持有人支付保费或者有实质性义务向保单持有人提供保险合同服务的，该权利或义务所产生的现金流量在合同边界内。
Portfolio of Insurance Contracts Portfolio /pɔːtˈfəʊliəʊ əv ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈkɒntrækts/
保险合同组合
A set of insurance contracts that are subject to similar risks and are managed together as a single pool.
由具有相似风险且一起管理的合同组成的保险合同集合。
Group of Insurance Contracts Group /gruːp əv ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈkɒntrækts/
保险合同组
A subdivision of a portfolio into at least three groups: (a) contracts initially recognized as onerous; (b) contracts with no significant possibility of becoming onerous; (c) remaining contracts. Contracts issued more than one year apart cannot be in the same group.
将一个保险合同组合至少分为三组：(a)初始确认时存在亏损的合同组；(b)初始确认时无显著可能性会在之后变为亏损合同的合同组；(c)剩余合同组成的合同组。签发时间间隔超过一年的合同不得归入同一组。
Onerous Contract /ˈɒnərəs ˈkɒntrækt/
亏损合同
A contract for which the total of the fulfilment cash flows, any previously recognized acquisition cash flows, and any cash flows arising at the date of initial recognition is a net outflow.
于初始确认日，分摊到一项保险合同的履约现金流、此前已确认的获取现金流及当日产生的现金流之总和为净现金流出的合同。
Loss Component LC /lɒs kəmˈpəʊnənt/
亏损部分
The portion of the liability for remaining coverage that represents the loss recognized for onerous contracts at initial recognition or in subsequent measurement.
初始确认和后续计量过程中确认的损失部分。
Initial Acquisition Cash Flows IACF /ɪˈnɪʃəl ˌækwɪˈzɪʃən kæʃ fləʊz/
保险获取现金流
Cash flows arising from the sale, underwriting and initiation of a group of insurance contracts that are directly attributable to the portfolio of insurance contracts.
因销售、核保和承保已签发或预计签发的合同组而产生的，可直接归属于其对应合同组合的现金流量。
Experience Adjustment /ɪkˈspɪəriəns əˈdʒʌstmənt/
经验调整
(a) For premiums received: the difference between the expected amount at the beginning of the period and the actual cash flow amount; (b) For insurance service expenses: the difference between the expected amount and the actual amount incurred.
(a)就收到的保费而言，期初预计的当期金额与当期实际现金流量金额的差额；(b)就保险服务费用而言，期初预计的当期发生额与当期实际发生额的差额。
Reinsurance Contract /ˌriːɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈkɒntrækt/
再保险合同
An insurance contract issued by one entity (the reinsurer) to compensate another entity for losses arising from one or more insurance contracts issued by the latter (the underlying contracts).
由一个主体（再保险人）签发的、对另一个主体因后者签发的一项或多项保险合同（标的合同）所产生的赔付进行补偿的保险合同。
Investment Contract with Discretionary Participation Features DPF /ɪnˈvestmənt ˈkɒntrækt wɪð dɪsˈkreʃənəri pɑːˌtɪsɪˈpeɪʃən ˈfiːtʃəz/
具有相机参与分红特征的投资合同
A financial instrument that grants the holder the right to receive additional amounts based on the returns of a specified pool of items, at the discretion of the issuer.
赋予特定投资者合同权利收取额外金额的金融工具，该额外金额由企业基于特定项目回报相机决定。
Insurance Contract with Direct Participation Features /ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈkɒntrækt wɪð daɪˈrekt pɑːˌtɪsɪˈpeɪʃən ˈfiːtʃəz/
具有直接参与分红特征的保险合同
An insurance contract that simultaneously meets three conditions: (a) the contract terms specify that the policyholder participates in a share of a clearly identified pool of underlying items; (b) the entity expects to pay a substantial share of the fair value returns to the policyholder; (c) a substantial proportion of the variation in amounts paid varies with the fair value of underlying items.
同时满足三个条件的保险合同：(a)合同条款规定保单持有人参与分享清晰可辨认的基础项目；(b)企业预计将基础项目公允价值变动回报中的相当大部分支付给保单持有人；(c)预计应付金额变动中的相当大部分随基础项目公允价值变动而变动。

Actuarial Measurement / 精算计量

General Measurement Model / Building Block Approach BBA /ˈdʒenərəl ˈmeʒəmənt ˈmɒdəl/
通用模型（要素法）
The default measurement model for insurance contracts under IFRS 17. It measures insurance liabilities as the sum of: (a) fulfilment cash flows (FCF = PV of future cash flows + risk adjustment) and (b) contractual service margin (CSM).
IFRS17的默认计量模型，适用于非分红保险合同（或具有间接分红特征保险合同）。保险合同负债 = 履约现金流（FCF = 未来现金流现值 + 风险调整）+ 合同服务边际（CSM）。
Under BBA: Insurance Liability = FCF + CSM = (PVCF + RA) + CSM
Variable Fee Approach VFA /ˈveəriəbl fiː əˈprəʊtʃ/
浮动收费法
The measurement model for insurance contracts with direct participation features. Under VFA, changes in the entity's share of the fair value of underlying items are adjusted through the CSM rather than recognized in profit or loss immediately.
适用于具有直接参与分红特征保险合同的计量模型。VFA下，基础项目公允价值中主体享有份额的变动通过调整CSM处理，而非立即计入损益。
VFA: FCF = UI - ES + NU; CSM absorbs changes in the entity's share of underlying items.
Premium Allocation Approach PAA /ˈpriːmiəm ˌæləˈkeɪʃən əˈprəʊtʃ/
保费分配法
A simplified measurement model applicable when: (a) the coverage period of each contract is one year or less; or (b) the entity can reasonably expect that the PAA result would not differ materially from the BBA result.
简化计量模型，适用于：(a)合同组内各项合同的责任期不超过一年；或(b)企业能够合理预计PAA与BBA计量结果无重大差异。
Fulfilment Cash Flows FCF /fʊlˈfɪlmənt kæʃ fləʊz/
履约现金流
An explicit, unbiased and probability-weighted estimate of the present value of future net cash flows that the entity expects to incur as it fulfils insurance contract obligations, including a risk adjustment for non-financial risk.
公司履行保险合同义务时发生的未来净现金流现值的显性、无偏、概率加权估计（即期望金额），包含对于非金融风险的风险调整。BBA下：FCF = RA + PVCF；VFA下：FCF = UI - ES + NU。
Contractual Service Margin CSM /kənˈtræktʃuəl ˈsɜːvɪs ˈmɑːdʒɪn/
合同服务边际（剩余边际）
A component of the carrying amount of a group of insurance contracts representing the unearned profit that the entity will recognize as it provides services in the future. CSM cannot be negative.
保险合同组的资产或负债的组成部分，反映主体将在未来提供服务时确认的未赚利润。CSM不得为负。
At initial recognition: if FCF + RA < 0, CSM = -(FCF + RA) (profitable contract); if FCF + RA >= 0, CSM = 0 (onerous contract, loss recognized immediately).
Risk Adjustment for Non-Financial Risk RA /rɪsk əˈdʒʌstmənt fɔː nɒn-faɪˈnænʃəl rɪsk/
非金融风险调整
The compensation that the entity requires for bearing the uncertainty about the amount and timing of the cash flows that arise from non-financial risk as it fulfils insurance contracts.
企业在履行保险合同时，因承担非金融风险导致的未来现金流量在金额和时间方面的不确定性而要求得到的补偿。
Common RA methods include Cost of Capital (CoC), Confidence Interval, and Conditional Tail Expectation (CTE).
Present Value of Future Cash Flows / Best Estimate Liability PVCF / BEL /ˈpreznt ˈvæljuː əv ˈfjuːtʃə kæʃ fləʊz/
预期现金流的现值（最优估计准备金）
The present value of expected future cash flows, including: (a) the time value of money and financial risk adjustment; (b) estimates of future cash flows (including TVOG for participating contracts).
包含：(a)货币时间价值及金融风险调整；(b)预期未来现金流（若具有分红特征，通常含选择权及保证利益的时间价值TVOG）。
Time Value of Options and Guarantees TVOG /taɪm ˈvæljuː əv ˈɒpʃənz ænd ˌɡærənˈtiːz/
选择权及保证利益的时间价值
The value of potential cash flows arising from options and guarantees in participating insurance, universal life, and variable annuity products.
对于分红保险、万能保险和变额年金保险等业务选择权及保证利益所可能产生的现金流。
Coverage Unit CU /ˈkʌvərɪdʒ ˈjuːnɪt/
保障责任单元
The unit used for amortizing the contractual service margin. The number of coverage units in a group is determined by considering the quantity of coverage provided by each contract and the expected coverage period.
用作合同服务边际摊销的单元。合同组中的保障责任单元的数量为合同组中的合同所提供的保障的数量，通过考虑每项合同所提供的利益及其预期保险责任期限确定。
Underlying Items UI /ˌʌndəˈlaɪɪŋ ˈaɪtəmz/
基础项目
Items that determine the amounts that the entity must pay to the policyholder. A pool of underlying items can include any item, such as a reference asset portfolio, the entity's net assets, or a specified portion, as long as the contract clearly identifies them. UI = VF + FCF.
用于确定某些应付保单持有人金额的项目。基础项目池可包含任何项目，例如参照资产组合、主体净资产或主体净资产之具体指明的一部分。UI = VF + FCF。
Variable Fee VF /ˈveəriəbl fiː/
未来服务的浮动费用
The variable fee that the entity charges for providing services under the contract in the future. At initial recognition, VF equals the opening CSM. VF = ES - NU.
未来因合同提供服务而收取的浮动费用。初始确认时，等于期初的CSM。VF = ES - NU。
The Entity's Share ES /ðə ˈentɪtiz ʃeə/
主体享有的基础项目公允价值份额的现值
The portion of the pool of underlying items that the entity retains, primarily consisting of fees charged and the entity's share of account returns (including interest spread, mortality margin, etc.).
基础项目池中主体所享有的部分，主要是一些主体从账户中收取的费用以及账户收益（包括利差、死差等）中主体享有的部分。
Discount Rate /ˈdɪskaʊnt reɪt/
折现率
A rate that reflects the time value of money, the characteristics of the insurance contract cash flows, and the liquidity characteristics of the contract. Under IFRS 17, the current discount rate is used for subsequent measurement (replacing the 750-day moving average under old rules).
反映货币时间价值、保险合同现金流量特征以及流动性特征的适当折现率。IFRS17下，后续计量采用当前折现率（替代原750天移动平均）。
Locked-in Rate LIR /lɒkt ɪn reɪt/
锁定利率
The discount rate determined at initial recognition of the contract, used for CSM interest accretion and adjustments. Under BBA, the locked-in rate is used for the CSM roll-forward.
合同初始确认时确定的折现率，用于CSM计息和调整项。BBA下，CSM滚动采用锁定折现率。
Current Rate /ˈkʌrənt reɪt/
当前利率
The discount rate based on current observable market data at the reporting date, used for measuring the fulfilment cash flows (PVCF and RA) in subsequent measurement.
基于报告日当前可观察市场数据确定的折现率，用于后续计量中履约现金流（PVCF和RA）的计量。
Top-Down Approach /tɒp daʊn əˈprəʊtʃ/
自上而下法
A method for determining the discount rate by starting with a risk-free yield curve and adjusting for the liquidity and credit characteristics of the reference portfolio.
确定折现率的方法之一，从无风险收益率曲线出发，调整参考组合的流动性和信用特征。
Bottom-Up Approach /ˈbɒtəm ʌp əˈprəʊtʃ/
自下而上法
A method for determining the discount rate by starting with the actual yield of the asset portfolio and adjusting to remove the effect of factors unrelated to the insurance liability cash flows.
确定折现率的方法之一，从资产组合的实际收益率出发，剔除与保险负债现金流量无关因素的影响。
Cost of Capital CoC /kɒst əv ˈkæpɪtl/
资本成本法
A method for calculating the risk adjustment by applying a cost-of-capital rate to the capital required to cover non-financial risk at a given confidence level.
计算风险调整的方法之一，对给定置信水平下覆盖非金融风险所需资本施加资本成本率。
RA = Required Capital × Cost of Capital Rate (e.g., 6%)
Conditional Tail Expectation CTE /kənˈdɪʃənl teɪl ɪkˌspekˈteɪʃən/
条件尾部期望
A risk measure calculated as the average of the worst (1-alpha)% of scenarios. For example, CTE(99%) is the average of the worst 1% of outcomes.
风险度量方法之一，计算为最差(1-alpha)%情景的平均值。例如CTE(99%)是最差1%结果的平均值。

Presentation / 报表列报

Liability for Remaining Coverage LRC /ˌlaɪəˈbɪlɪti fɔː rɪˈmeɪnɪŋ ˈkʌvərɪdʒ/
未到期责任负债
The portion of the insurance contract liability attributable to future service, comprising the fulfilment cash flows for future service and the contractual service margin at the reporting date.
当日分摊到该合同组的与未来服务有关的履约现金流，以及当日该合同组的合同服务边际。LRC = FCF(future) + CSM。
Liability for Incurred Claims LIC /ˌlaɪəˈbɪlɪti fɔːr ɪnˈkɜːd kleɪmz/
已发生赔款负债
The portion of the insurance contract liability attributable to past service, comprising the fulfilment cash flows for claims already incurred and related expenses.
当日分摊到该合同组的与过去服务（已发生赔案及其他相关费用）有关的履约现金流。
Insurance Service Revenue ISR /ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈsɜːvɪs ˈrevɪnjuː/
保险服务收入
Revenue recognized for providing insurance contract services, comprising: expected claims + expected maintenance expenses + acquisition cost amortization + CSM amortization + RA release. Excludes investment components.
提供保险合同服务确认的收入 = 预期理赔 + 预期维持费用 + RA提转差 + CSM释放 + 获取费用摊销。不含投资成分。
Insurance Service Revenue = Expected Claims + Expected Maintenance Expense + RA Release + CSM Amortization + Acquisition Cost Amortization
Insurance Service Expense /ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈsɜːvɪs ɪkˈspens/
保险服务费用
Expenses recognized for insurance contract services, including actual claims and related expenses incurred, acquisition cost amortization, and losses recognized/reversed for onerous contracts.
因当期发生赔案及其他相关费用导致已发生赔款负债的增加额，以及获取费用摊销、亏损部分的确认及转回等。不含投资成分。
Insurance Service Result ISR /ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈsɜːvɪs rɪˈzʌlt/
保险服务成果（承保利润）
Insurance Service Revenue minus Insurance Service Expense. Represents the underwriting profit/loss, excluding investment results.
保险服务收入减去保险服务费用。代表承保利润/亏损，不含投资成果。
Insurance Finance Income or Expenses /ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˈfaɪnæns ˈɪnkʌm ɔːr ɪkˈspensɪz/
保险财务损益
The portion of the change in insurance liabilities arising from the time value of money and changes in financial assumptions. Includes underwriting finance income/expenses and reinsurance finance income/expenses.
货币时间价值及金融风险的影响导致的保险合同负债变动额中计入当期损益的部分。包括承保财务损益和分出再保险财务损益。
Net Presentation /net ˌprezənˈteɪʃən/
净值列示
IFRS 17 requires that insurance contract assets and liabilities be presented on a net basis at the portfolio level. If the net carrying amount is a debit, it is presented as an asset; if a credit, as a liability.
IFRS17要求保险合同组合层面的资产和负债以净值列示。净额为借方余额的列示为保险合同资产，贷方余额的列示为保险合同负债。
Insurance Acquisition Cash Flow Asset /ɪnˈʃʊərəns ˌækwɪˈzɪʃən kæʃ fləʊ ˈæset/
保险获取现金流资产
An asset recognized for insurance acquisition cash flows paid or payable before the related group of contracts is recognized, systematically allocated to the relevant portfolio.
合同组确认前已付或应付的、系统合理分摊至相关合同组的保险获取现金流确认为资产。
Effective Yield Approach EYA /ɪˈfektɪv jiːld əˈprəʊtʃ/
（浮动）实际收益率法
A method that amortizes the updated total IFIE balance over the remaining coverage period at a single rate (similar to the expected yield to maturity).
将更新后的预期IFIE总余额按单一利率（类似于剩余期内的预计到期收益率）分摊至合同剩余期内各期。
Sensitivity Analysis /ˌsensɪˈtɪvɪti əˈnæləsɪs/
敏感性分析
A disclosure requirement showing the effect of reasonably possible changes in insurance risk variables and market risk variables on profit or loss and equity.
披露要求，反映资产负债表日保险风险变量和各类市场风险变量发生合理、可能的变动时，对企业损益和所有者权益产生的影响。
